# The economy, coarse-grained, end to end

Every market in the settled systems (the gate network: 36 places in 5 systems) is now a
place with people and works, and a stock of each kind of goods. Nothing appears or vanishes
at a market any more: goods are made, used up, and carried between places by ships.

- **What a place is** follows from where it is: a station (factories, an electronics fab, a
  fuel plant, a pharma plant, a smelter for asteroid ore); a farm world (an Earth-like world's
  spaceport: farms, artisans); a mining world (a dry world's: mines, a refinery, a chemical
  plant); an outpost (a cratered moon's: a mine, ice works).
- **Works** turn inputs into outputs (in tonnes a day, coarse recipes), as far as their inputs
  last and their storage has room; **people** use a basket of goods a day. Every 10 game
  minutes.
- **Markets are the places' economies.** A place sells what it makes (from its real stock)
  and buys what it uses (as far as it has room), a few varieties of each kind listed, and any
  variety of what it uses wanted. Prices follow its stock against ten days' use or output:
  short is dear (up to 3x), a glut cheap (down to 0.4x). Every trade puts tonnes in or takes
  them out. Asteroid ore sells to stations (their smelters) and mining worlds; ice to the
  places that use water.
- **Balanced coarsely**: every kind is made at 1.15–5.6x what's used across the settled
  places (fuel at 2.4x, with room for ships' fuel to come).
- Unsettled systems (beyond the gate network) keep their generated markets.

Instruments:
- `cargo run -p universe-sim --release --example economy [-- days]`: the balance table;
  `days` with no ship calling (everything runs short from day 10, farms included — their
  fields need chemicals from the mining worlds); and with an ideal hauler (nothing short; it
  takes about 1,600 t a day carried, about 80 holds of 20 t).
- **5: the economy panel**: across all places, made / used / short a day and what's short;
  every place with its kind, people and what it's shortest of; the one under the cursor in
  full — stock, days of cover, price against usual, made / used / short per kind.

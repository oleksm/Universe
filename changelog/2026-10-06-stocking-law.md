# Markets stock by the law; the laser's reset from its record

Merged fso (the stocking law, the laser's reset) and aligned the game with it:

- **A market aims to hold for its people what the administration's stocking law says**
  (`org.<administration>.compulsory_stock`, `settlement.resupply`): `warehouse_cover` (1.5) of its
  resupply interval of what its people take, need by need (food as any food); and, where the law
  holds a reserve, its share of what all the system's people would take over the reserve's time
  (Treistun: 120 days of food and 90 of medicine for everyone, half at Port Eikir, half at Port
  Nacaubun). Its prices follow its stock against that aim, beside what its works take: short, dear.
- **So food has a price to haul by:** from day 0, the economy alone, flour at Port Eikir asks 31
  cr/t by day 20 while the hungry ports bid 75, and 121 by day 40. Traders choose their runs by such
  margins.
- **The laser's reset is its record's** (`function.reset`): no weapon figure is left in the code.
- The economy test checks the law's reserve and that a market short of food bids more.

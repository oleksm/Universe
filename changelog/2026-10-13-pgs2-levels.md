# PGS1 revision 5 levels: tolerance reader and residency

- `worlds::pgs::Surface::query_to(direction, tolerance_m)` (and `query_differential_to`): a
  revision-5 package answers a visual query from the coarsest representation (root, a level,
  level 0) whose declared deviation is at or below the tolerance; nothing finer is opened.
  Tolerance 0 is revision 4 exactly. Composed ids follow the mixture the tolerance names.
- Level files: eleven header strings, kind 20 (level point → level-0 added point), sections
  ascending with the block hashes last; manifest levels refused when out of order, deviations
  decreasing, or the root's below the last level's.
- `Surface::verify_levels` (lab): level points byte-identical to level 0, deviation re-measured at
  the overlay's vertices. Examples `pgs2_level_goldens` (planets' pgs2-v2-levels: 2,508 of 2,508,
  5 of 5 refused) and `pgs2_levels` (a package's levels).
- The game draws a canonical surface to half a pixel (1280 px across 60°): a ground patch at
  `DRAWN_TOLERANCE_RAD` × the nearest distance it is drawn from, the globe map to half a texel,
  the sky's ground under the eye to half a pixel at its altitude. Physics and the contact group
  stay at tolerance 0.
- Residency: region files untouched for 20 s, or past a 256 MB resident budget, have their pages
  released (madvise and fadvise DONTNEED); a released file checks its blocks again on next read.

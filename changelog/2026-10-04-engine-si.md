# The engine takes the registry's units

- **Facilities in SI:** what a facility makes, takes in, gives off and burns is kg/s, its power W,
  its store kg (they were t/h, MW and t, converted from the records). The land office turns flows
  into tonnes where the market counts them, and power into MWh where it bills. The figures in play
  are the same.
- **Equipment in SI:** a hyperdrive's top speed is m/s (it was a multiple of c), a comm's or
  relay's capacity is messages a second (it was an hour). The records' figures are taken as they
  are.
- **Kept in their own scale:** the galaxy is laid out in light years (and a gate's span, measured
  against it), and a star's mass and light in Suns, as the system generator works. They're scales
  of the engine's own maps, converted once where a record enters them.

# One id for a ship (audit phase 5, #7)

- `protocol::ShipId`, in place of `usize` (and the misnamed `BodyId`) wherever a ship is meant:
  the player's is `ShipId::PLAYER` (0), craft `i`'s `ShipId::craft(i)`, a port's turret
  `ShipId::turret(system, k)` (from `ShipId::TURRETS`). `craft_index()` says which craft (none
  for the player's or a turret: a turret's id can no longer index the crafts by mistake).
  Written as its number (`serde(transparent)`), so input logs and replays read as before.
- Through the world (weapons, missiles, turrets, radar blips, damage, `ShipEvent::Hit`/`Collided`),
  services (ledger parties, law, traffic control's pads, corridors and queues, kill and trade
  records), avionics (fire-control tracks, hunters' sightings, gunners' quarry, follow anchors, the
  bus's id, the contact lock), sim (pilot view, postings, recorder, standings, commerce, order,
  estate) and the client (`Who::of`, sparks, sounds, the lock picker).
- Contacts were keyed by craft index and converted (`id - 1`, `craft_id(c)`) on the way to
  transponders and follow anchors. Blips, the lock, transponders and anchors now hold the ship's
  id, and those conversions are gone.
- Physics keeps its own opaque indices; the world converts at that edge.
- Checked in a frame: the orbit scenario's lock on a miner, its box and readout as before.

# The near ground changes without popping

- **Geomorphing** (the lab's R6): each patch vertex carries its way to its parent's shape and the
  patch's size (its vertex colour, otherwise unused on a patch); the mesh shader blends it toward
  that shape as the eye nears the distance where the parent stands in (`GEOMORPH_SPLIT`, kept with
  `terrain_lod::SPLIT`), so a split or a merge finds the same shape. A vertex on the parent's grid
  stays; one between takes the mean of the parent's either side (the cell's diagonal as cut).
- **A world's maps fade in** over a second of frames once bound (`WorldBind::fade`,
  `Globals::view.y`): their colour and the ground's material came in in one frame (grey rock to
  brown soil at 36 N 35 E, the whole view at once).
- Measured over 600 consecutive frames low over Harvest at 200 m/s: no frame changes more than a
  trace of the ground from the one before; the maps arrive and fade in over about 60 frames.
- Dev: `UNIVERSE_NO_WORLD_MAPS` draws without a world's full-resolution maps (to compare).

# Locking by mode

T locks what the mode is about. In navigation, everything within the sensors' reach (500 km):
ships, asteroids big and small (remnants and swarm fragments), stations, gates, spaceports —
nearest first in the list; a station, gate or port locked becomes the nav target, a rock the
rock lock (N/U keep at range from it or orbit it). In combat, the ships. In mining, the rocks
the prospect found. A tap locks what's nearest the nose within the beam, of what the mode
offers.

- **The lock beam shows** as a ring round the nose while T is down (and a moment after): a tap
  locks what's within it (6°, in every mode); held, T lists everything within reach, in any
  direction.
- **Sensor reach grows with size**, as the radar equation has it (the fourth root of the
  cross-section): a ship at 500 km, a 20 m boulder at about 570 km, a station about 1,300 km,
  a kilometre-wide rock about 1,500 km, a port's pads about 1,100 km.

# The sun as a camera sees it

- **The star's surface white-hot,** no grid lines on it (they read as a black mesh over the core):
  it blooms in the tone curve.
- **The glare falls away smoothly with angle:** seven layered glows, each twice as wide and half
  as strong (no edge, no smoky disc), white near the sun, its colour further out.
- **Streaks:** thirty-two fine ones, uneven, every eighth longer, fading out.
- **Lens ghosts:** faint coloured discs on the line from the sun through the middle of the view,
  stronger as the sun nears the middle.
- **The core** (in the scene, hidden by what's in front) white-hot.
- **Space dust at 20%** opacity.

## Follow-up

- **The glare is tighter:** five layers, each 1.7× wider and 0.4× as strong; the veil a quarter as strong.
  It no longer fogs the whole view.
- **The canopy follows the light:** its own glow cut to a trace; it glints in the sun and is dark in shade.

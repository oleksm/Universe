# Paint, and a hauler with form

- **Ships painted by trade:**
  - ours white with a deep blue stripe;
  - traders warm off-white and orange;
  - pirates gunmetal and red;
  - miners industrial yellow with a black stripe;
  - shuttles white and blue;
  - settlers light grey and teal.
- **How the paint reads:**
  - each panel its own shade (plates fitted one by one);
  - the bevel strips darker (the gaps);
  - an accent band round the hull and on the outermost tips;
  - the engine end sooted toward the nozzles.
  - The hull is cut into slabs at the paint lines, so a band crosses even a panel that runs the
    ship's length.
- **The bulk hauler reshaped** (a brick before):
  - an octagonal hull, its bow tapering up to a bridge tower;
  - thrusters, lift and gear moved onto the new surfaces; balance and room checked by the tests;
  - frames round the hold every 9 m standing proud of the hull (on any hull over 60 m long).
- **Engine bells no wider than the gap between them** (the hauler's four overlapped).
- **Canopies seated on the hull:** each corner on the surface, narrowing to fit (they floated
  over the drover's ridge).
- **White strobes** on the wing tips (a double flash) and the tail, instead of red and green.
- **Space dust** back: a twelfth as dense, faint.
- Dev scenario `showship` (`UNIVERSE_HULL`, `UNIVERSE_YAW`, `UNIVERSE_DIST`): our ship as any
  hull, in sunlight, seen from the side.

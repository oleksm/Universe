# Light, revised

- **Brightness follows the light more closely.** What a face is lit by goes as the star's
  irradiance (luminosity over distance squared) to the 0.45 power — the eye adapts less than
  before (0.3) — and up to 4x the light at 1 AU from a sun-like star (was 1.6): near a star
  colours wash out to white; far out (5 AU) everything is darker.
- **The sun dazzles by how much light there is.** Its halo and rays grow with the square root
  of the irradiance (ten times the glare at a hundred times the light), and looking toward it
  washes the whole view out — faintly at 1 AU, blinding at 0.1 AU.
- **Rocks reflect by their class**: C-types are coal-dark (albedo 0.05), stony 0.22, metallic
  0.15, icy bright (0.6) — shown by the square root, as the eye takes it.
- Dev scenario `sunclose`: a tenth of an AU from the star, facing it.

# Balance: where the mass is

A ship's centre of mass is now where its mass really is, not the middle of its shape:

- **The frame** is spread through the shape.
- **Each module sits at its mount** on the hull (`mount_<slot>` nodes): the drive and power plant
  aft, the computers and guns in the nose, the mining rig on the spine.
- **Fuel sits in the tank's place, cargo in the hold's** (capacity-weighted between racks).
- So the centre of mass moves as you load cargo, burn fuel or refit, and the inertia follows
  (each mass about the centre of mass: parallel axis).

**What off balance costs:**
- The thrusters push about the centre of mass where it is now. When the thrust line misses it,
  pushing straight takes the opposing thrusters, so less push is left.
- `ClassSpec::authority`: the most the drive, the lift and the thrusters can give without
  turning the ship, by more than 3% of what it can turn by. It's worked out per tonne of fuel
  and of load (a tonne either way changes little), computed at that tonne exactly, so the world
  stays the same to the bit whichever thread asks first.
- The flight computer asks for no more than that: off balance, a ship pushes less but stays
  straight. The autopilots plan with it (`main_accel`, `lift_accel`, `side_accel`).

**Stock hulls are built balanced** about their usual load: a full tank, the hold half full.
- Their thruster layouts are centred on that point.
- From an empty to a full hold they keep 92–100% of their push (the Drover's lift 94%).
- The Drover's hold is forward: a full hold moves its centre of mass 2 m forward.
- Refits shift the balance: a lighter drive in the tail moves the centre of mass forward.

**Where you see it:**
- The HUD warns "OFF BALANCE - LIFT 82% DRIVE 100%" when the lift or drive is under 90%, in red
  under 75% (a stock ship keeps 92% and more, empty to full).
- The shipyard shows BALANCE for the ship now and with the module picked (lift and drive, empty
  hold / full hold).

**Solver:** the authority is worked out to convergence (twelve passes from cold), which the light
interceptor needed. In flight the solver starts from the last frame's settings, as before.

**Tests:**
- every hull keeps over 90% (drive, lift, thrusters) at its usual load, empty and full;
- loading the Drover moves its centre of mass forward;
- 3 m off balance, its lift drops under 85%.

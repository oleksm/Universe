# The galaxy map as a galaxy

- **The galaxy as light:** every star's glow gathered once on a grid over the plane (256², softened),
  toned like a photograph:
  - warm white in the bulge, bluish white in the disc;
  - pink where stars bunch in the arms;
  - drawn as a smooth image (colour per cell corner).
- **Stars:** a sprinkle of fine single-pixel points over the glow far out; closer, soft glowing discs
  in their class's colour. The glow fades as the stars come apart.
- **Explored systems** glow white-hot; **YOU** is a fine ring with its label under it.
- **Opens at 50 ly** from us to the top edge (the neighbourhood and the settled network), not the
  whole galaxy.
- The scale bar no longer runs into the controls. The `galaxymap` scenario takes `UNIVERSE_ZOOM`.

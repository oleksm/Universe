# The insurer from its record

- **Treistun Mutual's terms** (`world::order::insurer`, `org.treistun-mutual.insurance`) replace the
  game's constant: the excess is the record's share (a tenth) of the ship's worth, paid to the
  insurer (a ledger company, `LandOffice::enlist`).
- **Delivered at a yard, parked** (`delivered_at: yard`): a lost ship comes back on a pad at Trethi
  Yard's port (Port Trethi, on Rime), not in flight by the station. Nobody is put back into the fight.
- **Losses it refuses** (`refuses`): a loss brought on by piracy (fair game for firing on the
  innocent at the time) gets nothing from it: the basic ship, nothing charged.
- Giving a ship up (`respawn`) goes through the insurer too; tests and dev views that only want the
  ship in flight by the station use `start_in_flight`.
- `Event::Insured` says what was refused and where the ship is parked.
- Not yet: the premium (3% a year), and the other refused offences (murder, smuggling, reckless
  flying) until the law records offences.

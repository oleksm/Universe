## Avionics (`follow`)

- **Keep at range**: the ship holds station at a set range from its anchor, wherever the anchor
  goes. It closes or opens the range as fast as it can still brake to a stop at the right point
  (300 m/s at most), and matches the anchor's velocity once there.
- **Orbit**: the ship circles its anchor at a set radius:
  - the orbit's plane is the one the ship is already moving in, so it starts smoothly;
  - the speed is set so the turn takes 40% of the thrusters, up to 150 m/s (69 m/s at 2 km);
  - the radius and the plane are held while the anchor moves.
- **Anchors**:
  - a ship: as the radar measures it, so the follow ends if the ship goes out of radar range,
    jumps, docks, lands or is destroyed;
  - a station or a gate: from the charts.
- **Ranges**: 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 and 20 km. The closest allowed is 500 m from a ship, or 1 km beyond
  a station's or gate's structure.
- **Flown like a pilot would fly it**:
  - nose on the anchor, with the thrusters doing small corrections;
  - for bigger corrections it turns and burns the main engine (the same law as the hunter's
    `thrust_for`);
  - feedforward of the anchor's own acceleration (estimated by a fire-control `Track`), less the
    gravity on the ship. So it falls with the anchor and burns only for the difference;
  - `hunter::avoid` keeps it off the ground, stations and gates.
- **It ends when**:
  - the dock/land autopilot or the route autopilot takes over;
  - the hyperdrive engages;
  - the ship lands, crashes or leaves the system;
  - the anchor is lost.
- Crafts can run it too (`Universe::craft_follow`), which is ready for escorts later.

## Game

- **N**: keep at range from the locked ship (T), or else from the nav target if it's a station
  or gate. **U**: orbit it.
- The first press picks the preset range nearest where you are. Pressing the same key again
  steps to the next range out. **X** releases.
- Action grid cells: `N KEEP` and `U ORBIT`, showing the range when engaged (e.g. `ORBIT 5K`).
- HUD line: `ORBIT 5 KM - <NAME>  NOW 7.0 KM`.
- Refusals now show their own reason, no longer prefixed with "CLEARANCE DENIED".
- Dev scenario `orbit`.

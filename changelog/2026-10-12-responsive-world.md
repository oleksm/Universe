World visuals no longer block the first game update: terrain globes/maps/lines use the engine's bounded asynchronous scheduler, leaving base geometry visible while pending. Initial ground patches also prepare on workers. Globe texture mip preparation happens on workers and GPU uploads are limited to 2 MiB per frame; partial layers are never sampled. Active frame leases protect CPU residency, old view leases are released, and stale queued work is cancelled. Globe and terrain workers use bounded parallelism. Renderer scopes distinguish mesh/PBR/globe uploads, surface acquisition, canvas encoding and submission. Deliberately delayed loading validation accompanies this change.

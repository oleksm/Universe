# Made things are marked (SFO 21)

- Every whole unit of a product the economy finishes (a hull, equipment, an industrial module, a
  structure, a part) gets its mark: the serial (its maker's ticker, the product's code from its
  key, the next number in that series: `TOL-MC07-01-000001`), its design, its maker (the works'
  owning company), where (the works' key) and when (the step's world time: its life begins
  there), and the serials of the marked parts that went into it. Bulk stock comes in lots
  (`TOL-000042`), a works' day of an item each.
- Marks and lots lie in the pool with the stock (`Pool::marks`, `Pool::lots`), oldest first; they
  go with it when works and the warehouse trade (`take_with`, `put_with`), and a series goes on
  from day 0's serials (the Trethi yard's hulls). A works with no company to own it makes
  unmarked.
- Not yet: carried into a ship's hold or onto a ship as it's fitted, marks on the seeding's and
  fleets' hulls, and life counted from `made_on` (wear isn't in the game yet).
- Cost: an economy step 0.99 → 1.18 ms (a lot moved in pieces joins itself; a works keeps its
  open lot, else it opened one a step).
- The yard test (it stepped weeks of the economy at its 10 s: up to 345,600 steps since the
  clocks) dropped, by the user's word.
  The suite: 29 s.
- The maker is the owner of the parcel a works stands on (the Scientist's rule; facilities have no
  owner of their own), a rig's owner; the administration is an organisation like any other
  (org.treistun: TRS). All 57 works resolve to a maker with a ticker: no unit goes unmarked.
- Merged with it: the audit's step 2 (rules as records: `rule`, and a basis entry may name one).
- Carried onto ships: what a pilot buys at a market goes into the hold with its marks and lots
  (`Ledger::load_marked`), and back with them when sold; a module fitted takes its mark onto the
  ship and one taken out gives it back (`Ship::marks`); a hull bought from stock comes with its
  mark. Not yet exercised by a test: no market sells a marked hull at day 0 (the yard's haulers
  aren't what its lines make, so it keeps them).

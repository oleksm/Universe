# Hitches written down; a frame-time graph

- **Hitch log:** a frame over 50 ms is written to `hitches.log` beside the quicksave (and the
  log): how long, its parts (update, draw, hand-over; the render thread's work and its wait
  for the screen), and with the profiler on (F3 twice, or `UNIVERSE_PROFILE=1`) every scope's
  time in it, the slowest first. A stall's cause, kept after it's gone.
- **Frame-time graph** (F3): the last 300 frames as bars (green under 1/60 s, amber slower,
  red a hitch), the worst of them and the hitches so far.
- First catch (on foot by the ship): building ground patches 22 ms in one frame, uploading
  vertices 16 ms, the galaxy's sky 11 ms; on the sim thread, 987 parked ships 23 ms a tick.

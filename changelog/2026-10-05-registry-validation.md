# Registry validation: trim and fit

*Registry only (`fso`).*

- **Every record names its own schema** on its first line; one that names none fails.
- **A short checker with a standard library** (`tools/standards/check.py`): open each record, find
  its schema, validate. It passes the whole registry in three seconds.
- **Physical things derive from one base:** a kind that exists in the world must have its physical
  figures, each above nothing. Companies, laws and needs do not.
- 54 missing figures filled, as guesses marked to review.

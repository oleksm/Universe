# 2026-09-30 — Hyperdrive drops out at the top of the air

User: "why it does not let me come closer to 700 km from a planet on a hyper? I thought we wanted
to clear it up to the atmosphere level"

- **Before.** Flying the drive at a planet or moon with no destination, it dropped out at a
  fixed `PLANET_MARGIN` of 1,000 km (or 10% of the radius), set when spaceports came in. The
  hard limit (the interlock) was always only 1 km above the highest ground.
- **Now.** It drops out at the top of the body's air, or `GROUND_MARGIN` (20 km) above its
  highest ground if that's higher (airless bodies: 20 km above their highest ground). Stars
  keep their 3-radius margin.
- **Test.** `untargeted_the_drive_drops_out_at_the_top_of_the_air`: flying straight at the
  nearest planet with air, it drops out at 146 km, with the air's top at 149 km.

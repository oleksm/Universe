# 2026-09-30 — Lock by beam, and a fuller target readout

User: "locking system is not firing properly. Pressing T should search for targets within the
beam of the crosshair's extended circle. The locked target should show name, distance, angular
velocity, directional vector. In combat mode it also shows health."

- **T locks what's in the beam**: radar contacts within `LOCK_BEAM` = 6° of the nose. It picks
  the one nearest the crosshair; T again moves to the next in the beam, wrapping round. With only
  the locked one in the beam, T releases it. With nothing in the beam: "NO TARGET IN THE BEAM -
  PUT IT IN THE RING". (`Universe::lock_in_beam`; the old nearest-first cycling is kept for dev
  scenarios.)
- **The beam ring**: a cyan circle of 6° around the nose, with ticks. It's always shown in combat
  mode (outside the red 4° gimbal ring) and for 1.5 s after T in travel.
- **Locked readout**:
  - `LOCK <NAME>  <range>  CLOSING/OPENING <speed>`;
  - `ANG <deg/s>` (line-of-sight rate: how fast it crosses the view),
    `REL <speed>  FWD/RGT/UP <m/s>` (its velocity relative to us, in our ship's frame);
  - what it's doing and where it's bound;
  - in combat mode, `TARGET HULL [..] n%`. This is a combat scan's reading, added to `Contact`
    as `hull`.
- **Direction arrow** on the locked target's bracket: which way it's moving across the view,
  relative to us.
- Test: `t_locks_what_is_in_the_beam_nearest_the_crosshair` (picks the nearest to the crosshair,
  goes to the next, wraps round, and releases when turned away).

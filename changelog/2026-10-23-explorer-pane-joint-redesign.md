Explorer glazing now permits explicit pane-edge reconstruction after the preserved fifteen-pane prototype exposed 21 glass/glass conflicts. The chosen draft uses shared mullion datums, individually retained faceted panes, a defined front-header ownership boundary and a 3 mm minimum glass-edge gap where no fixed web requires more space. Original panes remain historical; changed quantities and hashes must be measured again. The failed prototype also has incomplete support/fastener rows and independent material intersections, so this decision grants no installed mass or release acceptance.

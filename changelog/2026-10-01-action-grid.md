# Modes on top, instruments below

- **Mode bar, top left**: NAV (the default), B COMBAT, 1 MINING, G MARKET, M MAP, TAB WATCH
  (observer), F1 HELP — the active one lit (combat amber while the weapons prime, red when
  hot). A mode's key again goes back to navigation. One mode at a time: into mining, the
  weapons go safe; into combat, mining mode ends. Anchored to a rock, it's mining.
- **Instruments, lower left**: the active mode's keys, under its name.
  - NAV: dock/land, auto, hyper, lock, keep, orbit, let go, collision warning, grid, view —
    docked: launch (or lift off), fly route, leave the seat; in hyperdrive: drop out, auto
    steer, speed.
  - COMBAT: gun, laser, lock, keep, orbit, let go, hyper, collision warning, view.
  - MINING: prospect, lock, approach, anchor (let go), dig (stop), keep, orbit, let go, hyper.
  - Gate transit, destroyed, on foot and observer have their own.
  Cells are lit in use, amber when busy, red when hot, dim when not available.
- The status lines start below the performance readout at the top right.

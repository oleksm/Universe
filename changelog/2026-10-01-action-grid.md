# Action grid by situation

The key grid moved to the lower left and shows what can be done in the situation the ship is
in, under its name: FLIGHT, COMBAT (gun, laser, lock, follow, safe), MINING (prospect, lock,
approach, anchor, dig, keep, orbit, let go), ANCHORED (dig/stop, let go, prospect, lock),
HYPERDRIVE (drop out, auto steer, speed), DOCKED / LANDED (launch or lift off, market, map and
route, fly route, leave the seat), GATE TRANSIT, DESTROYED (respawn), ON FOOT and OBSERVER.
Cells are lit in use, amber when busy, red when hot, dim when not available. The status lines
start lower, clear of the performance readout at the top right.

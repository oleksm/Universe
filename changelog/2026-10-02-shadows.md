# Shadows

- **Normals from the faces themselves.** The renderer used to turn each face's normal "away from
  the model's centre", which holds only for a ball.
  - On the platform station, the structure's face toward the deck got the far face's normal: it
    lit up with the sun behind it.
  - The same went for the gate ring's inner faces and parts of the winged hulls.
  - Now a face's normal is its winding (counter-clockwise from outside); the globe generator,
    which wound its faces inward, is turned round.
  - Edges are lit by the faces they bound, and detail lines by the face they lie on (not
    "away from the centre").
  - Tests: every model's faces (globes, hulls, station, gate, every hull) wind outward.
- **Eclipses.** Planets and moons hide the sun by the share of its disc they cover, seen from
  each lit thing (`Frame::sun_visible`, `disc_covered`): a ship on a night side or in a moon's
  shadow is in the dark, with a real penumbra.
- **Shadows cast by ships, stations, gates and rocks** on each other and themselves:
  - a shadow map from the sun, in two cascades round the eye (310 m and 2.5 km either way,
    2048² each), sampled per pixel by the mesh shaders, filtered at the edges;
  - it reaches 8 km sunward;
  - the cascades are held to whole texels so shadow edges don't crawl as you move;
  - globes don't cast (their nights are their shading; their eclipses above);
  - `UNIVERSE_SHADOW_DEBUG=1` tints what's in shadow red.
  - Checked in frames: `deckshadowside`, `deckshadowface` (new dev scenarios, the sun round the
    side of or in front of the structure).
  - The platform's deck faces its orbit's pole, so the sun is always low over it (a few
    degrees): long shadows, and with the sun behind the structure, the whole deck in its shadow.
- **The sun's glare** goes with how much of its disc is in sight: the planets' share exactly,
  the stations' structures by rays to points across the disc. It fades as the disc slides behind
  an edge instead of vanishing when the centre does.

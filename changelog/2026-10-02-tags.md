# Contact tags decluttered

- In the pilot's seat each radar contact is a small mark (fainter the farther). The nearest six get
  **one tag: name and range** (the name within 5 km), placed beside the mark, skipped where it would
  overlap another tag or the nav target's bracket and label. A crowd round a station shows its nearest,
  not a heap of text.
- The scene no longer writes ships' names in the pilot's seat (the tags carry them); elsewhere it does.

# Climbing out upright

- **A route's climb-out holds the ship level with the local vertical** (heading kept), not by a station.
  Its attitude used to go unheld: its up drifted off the vertical (0.44 by 9.5 km), the lift pushed it
  sideways at 400 m/s, and it topped out near 9.7 km and sank back. It only got away because the jump
  height was lower. Now it climbs straight, still accelerating, till clear. (`flies` counts the climb,
  so its steering is taken.)

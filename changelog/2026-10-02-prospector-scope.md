# A prospector with form, a new scope, calmer lights

- **The prospector reshaped:**
  - an octagonal hull widening to its engines, its bow tapered;
  - a drill boom under its nose, a dorsal block over the rig;
  - thrusters, lift and gear moved onto the new surfaces; balance, room and the designer's copy
    checked by the tests.
- **The radar scope restyled:**
  - a soft dark disc instead of a box;
  - faint range rings, the rim brighter with ticks round it, a sector ahead, our chevron;
  - contacts and bodies as small glowing points, their stalks firm above the plane, faint below;
  - the locked contact ringed.
- **Canopies larger** (a wider, taller glass wedge, seated on the hull), lit from inside so they
  read in shade.
- **Strobes calmer:** one short flash every three seconds or so, dimmer and smaller.
- `showship` takes `UNIVERSE_PITCH`.

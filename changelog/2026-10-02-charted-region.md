# The charted region: stars at real density

- **The galaxy's stars are now one region at real density:** a 200 ly cube in the outer disc,
  32,000 stars at the Sun's neighbourhood's density (one per about 250 ly³), neighbours 4-6 ly
  apart, by the real class mix (three in four red dwarfs). It was 40,000 stars over the whole
  19,000 ly disc, a median 41 ly apart. The galaxy beyond is the map's glow only, for now.
- **Gates now span 4-7 ly** (were 16-90): a Drover crosses in under a minute, as the tube formula
  intended.
- **Home is an Earth-like world:** under about 1.1 g, so a starter ship lands on it.
- **Galaxy map:** opens at a 5 ly scale bar on your neighbourhood; stars fade with height above or
  below your plane (a 40 ly slab round you); the region's edge drawn; lane lengths where they fit.
- **Old saves can't load** (their stars are other stars now): the save format is version 2.
- World article `docs/world/galaxy.md`: every number with its source or target.

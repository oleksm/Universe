# The NET badge

- **The hypernet status is a badge at the top right,** left of the credits: four signal bars
  and the lag. Green, all four, under a second; yellow, three, under a minute; red, one, past it;
  off the net, grey bars crossed and "NET OFF". (The NET row in the instruments went.)
- **Moved to the top line's left, without the word NET:** the bars and the lag ("0.07 S", "OFF").
  The top line is for status (the badge, the guidance banner when one's up); the buttons always
  start on the line under it, banner or not.

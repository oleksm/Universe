# Merged fso: what is sold is stock; supply for people's needs; mounts

- **What is sold is stock** (the user's rule, made strict in the registry): a good names no market
  category any more; each traded good has a stock item in bulk (`stock.wheat-bulk`) that does. In
  the game a good with a stock of its own in bulk **is** that stock, wherever it lies, is made or is
  carried (one item, not two: the catalogue folds `good.wheat` into `stock.wheat-bulk`). Markets list
  only stock and what's made of it (parts, equipment, hulls); bare goods, elements and materials
  aren't sold, though works still draw them from their settlement's store. Ore dug is its stock in
  bulk (`stock.stony-ore-bulk`). A good's category is its stock's.
- **Supply for people's needs is in the registry** (life support at the sealed ports, water works,
  Port Eikir's farms and food works, hydroponic halls, a month of food and oxygen and a week of
  water in store): the economy runs its new works. People don't consume yet (next).
- **Equipment has real shapes:** the Interceptor, an outdated stock hull, no longer has room for
  its life support. An outdated hull now flies crowded (its crowding said) instead of stopping the
  game from loading; every other hull must fit what it carries.
- Tests moved with it: ore saved as its stock, the refit test's mass against itself (the avionics
  weigh their real weights now), the room test past the outdated hulls.
- Mounts (SFO 19) are in the registry; the game fits by slot kind and size as before.

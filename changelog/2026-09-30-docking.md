# 2026-09-30 — Docking: find, request, guidance, docking computer

## Summary

Docking went from "touch an invisible sphere" to a full sequence: find the station, request
clearance, follow a 3D approach corridor by hand, or hand over to the docking computer.

## Player-facing

- **Finding the station**: a nav marker (diamond + range) on the station in pilot mode; when it's
  off-screen, an arrow at the screen edge points to it. A hint reminds you to press R.
- **R: request docking.** Granted within 50 km; denied with a reason otherwise (out of range, no
  station, in hyperdrive, not flying). Clearance lapses beyond 100 km.
- **Guidance**: a corridor of gates along the station's docking axis, shaped like the slot and
  rolling with it, flaring out to 4 km, plus a 3D cross at the approach point. Gates turn green when
  you're lined up (position and roll), amber when not; the next gate is highlighted.
- **Docking HUD**: phase (manual or AUTO APPROACH/ALIGN/FINAL), range, closing speed vs. the speed
  limit, axis offset, roll error in degrees, and a hint ("ROLL TO LINE WINGS UP WITH THE SLOT",
  "SLOW DOWN", "BEHIND THE STATION - GO AROUND", ...). Red when too fast or badly rolled.
- **K: docking computer.** Requests clearance if needed, then flies Approach (going around the
  station if you start behind it) → Align (hold at 4 km, match the station's spin) → Final (down the
  axis under the speed limit) → docked. Press K again to take back control. Time warp works while it
  flies (about 4 min of game time from spawn, ~25 s real at 10x).
- **Shift+W/S/A/D/Q/E: RCS translation thrusters** (6 m/s²) for fine manual control.
- **Docked**: the camera pulls back to show the station; W launches you out of the slot along the axis.

## Simulation

- New `docking` module in `crates/sim`: station geometry, clearance, guidance numbers, autopilot.
- The station's real shape is used for collision (a cuboctahedron: cube ∩ octahedron). You dock
  only by entering the slot (under 25 m/s, wings within 30° of the slot). Slow hull contact bounces
  you off; fast contact is fatal. Because the station spins (60 s), its side faces move at about
  50 m/s, so gentle bumps only happen near the spin axis.
- The ship gained RCS thrust and a `docking` state, both saved with the game.
- Physics takes 50 ms substeps near stations for precise control and contact.
- Tests: autodock from the spawn point, autodock from behind the station, slow bump vs. fast crash,
  launch clears the station.

## Dev

- New scenarios: `approach` (cleared, manual, 2.5 km out), `autodock` (computer mid-final),
  `docked`, `lost` (station off-screen).

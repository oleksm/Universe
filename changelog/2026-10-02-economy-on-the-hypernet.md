# The economy and the markets over the hypernet

- **Each place reports with its price board** (every 2 minutes): its stocks, people, what it makes
  and lacks. The world's first reports are long known everywhere.
- **The economy panel (N) shows each place as its report reached us:** its delay is the place's
  lag to its system's backbone, through the gate relays to ours, and out to us. A new AGE column
  ("42 S", "3 MIN", "LONG"; "NO WORD" off the net, showing what's long known); the place picked
  says how old its report is. (The WAIT column went: the place's details show it.)
- **The market screen** uses the same: a market elsewhere as its board reached us, now through the
  gate relays too.
- Dev scenario `economyheard`.

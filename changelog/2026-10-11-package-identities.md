# Package identities and transfer accounting

Generate runtime labels from the registry's label schema. Recipe outputs immediately fill their registry handling unit, before downstream consumption. Full units seal automatically; daily lot closure seals the true partial remainder. Package 9999 closes its lot and subsequent output gets a new lot.

Whole sealed units retain their labels on transfer. Splitting or consuming a unit opens it and drops its packaging while the stock retains its lot. The filling remainder stays at its works. Pools and holds account for historical tare and outer volume; market and internal transfer checks include packaging before booking payment. Production conservatively reserves shell weight in store capacity.

Exact ledger quantities and package metadata are exposed for save/restore; display rounding is not used for mass, persistence or clearing a hold. Ships owns the save integration. Drawn label textures remain a separate client integration; these changes do not claim visual delivery. No main merge.

Validation covers accrual, partial sealing, immutable transfer, split/repack, loose-first selection, suffix exhaustion, production consumption ordering, and the existing economy regression.

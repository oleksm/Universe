# What defines a body: the spec

- **The celestial body schema grows from 23 properties to 70**, in twelve groups: its orbit in full,
  what it is made of in bulk, its inside (core, mantle, crust, heat), its magnetic field, its crust
  (rocks, loose ground, ores, tectonics, volcanism, how old its surface is), its water, its surface
  extremes, its air (pressure, gases, whether it can be breathed), and what lives there. The new
  ones have no values yet: they are what the planet-evolution work and curation are to fill.
- **Worked out for every body** and shown on its page: density, sunlight, the temperature it would
  have with no air, the speed and energy to leave its surface for orbit and to leave it altogether,
  how fast its ground moves, and the orbit that keeps over one spot.

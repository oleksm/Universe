# The world at day 0: Trethi builds

Merged the registry's day 0 (the user's word): starting stock on 24 facilities, a population for
each settlement, `traded_as` on every good and stock item, the deuterium plant's water drawn in
place, `built_of.parts` on hulls, and the equipment figures the game held as constants.

- **Starting stock:** each works' store and each warehouse holds at day 0 what its record says
  (kg, or pieces). Every power station has a year's deuterium, every port's warehouse 100 t for
  ships; Trethi's foundry and smelter a week of what they take, its mill three days of ingot, its
  yard the bills of two MC-07s and their equipment.
- **Trethi Yard builds an MC-07 in 21.5 days** from that alone (the registry: 21.6), its company
  setting its modules. The test no longer brings any stock of its own.
- **Populations:** each settlement's people, from its record (220,000 across Treistun). What they
  eat and use isn't run yet.
- **Parts the registry hasn't described** (no mass, nothing they're made of: the MC-07's landing
  legs, ramp, clamps and mining laser) aren't built into a hull. They counted a tonne each before,
  and the dock waited for them for ever.
- **The company operator picks only what it has the makings of:** the assembly shop no longer sits
  on a drive whose shield it would have to make itself.
- **Power is asked for only by what can run:** a module with nothing to work on draws none, so the
  yard no longer runs at 94% for the smelter's idle lines.
- **A survey is the fitted sensor's:** how far it makes out a rock for its size and how far it
  looks are the radar's figures from the registry (`resolves`, `survey_range`), not constants. A ship
  with no such sensor surveys nothing.

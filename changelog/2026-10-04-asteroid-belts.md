# Asteroid belts described from what is known

- **How asteroids lie**, in the celestial registry, from the Sun's belts with sources: the main belt
  (about 1.2 million over 1 km, a million km apart, a third of them in some 25 families spread round
  their orbits), the trojan swarms, the outer belt, how many have moons (about 2%), and how their
  number rises as their size falls.
- **Each charted system's belts worked out** from those and its own planets: where each lies and
  about how many go round the star in it. Treistun's main belt: 0.71 to 1.12 AU, about 142,000 over
  1 km and 11 million over 100 m. The game's fields are filed under the belt each lies in.
- **The game's fields are named for what they are**: a remnant with a bound swarm, true to a body
  broken lately, a simplification of a belt. The engine is to follow the spec; `docs/registry-
  integration.md` says what that asks of it.

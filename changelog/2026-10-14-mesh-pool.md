# The mesh pool: many small meshes, indexed, in one buffer, drawn at once

*2026-10-14, engine. Asked by planets (ask 5 of `docs/planet-lod-app.md` on engine-claude-planetlod): the
planet LOD app's cluster mode draws 2,000 to 6,000 meshes of about 128 triangles a frame, and its cost was
uploading them (56 B a corner) and one draw call each.*

- `PoolModel` (positions, faces and edges by index; optional normals, vertex colours, per-face colours, a
  per-vertex morph and band) packs to a `PooledMesh` on any thread: about 12-20 B a vertex, two bytes an
  index (four past 65,536 vertices), four a face colour.
- The renderer keeps one pool buffer (from 4 MB, doubling to 1 GB as the GPU allows), each mesh a range
  of it: copied in (a `write_buffer`) the first frame it is drawn or `Frame::prepare_pooled`, let go when
  its last copy is dropped. `PooledMesh::gpu_resident` says when it is in.
- `Frame::pool_group(transform, line, fill, edges, lit)` then `Frame::pooled(group, mesh, origin)`: every
  pooled mesh of a layer is one draw for faces and one for edges. The vertex shader (`vs_pool`) finds its
  draw by its corner (a binary search), reads its indices and vertices from the pool and goes through the
  meshes' own vertex code, so lighting and the fragment shader are the same as a plain mesh's. Faces are
  flat unless the mesh is smooth (the face's normal from its three corners); a face colour overrides its
  vertices'.
- Morph: each vertex moves by its offset as the eye's distance goes from its band's near to far, faces
  and edges alike (planets' ask 1, for pooled meshes).
- Not yet: pooled meshes cast no shadows; no globe maps on them.
- `Resources` (the observer's) has the pool's meshes, size and used bytes.
- Checked with `examples/pool_fixture.rs`: the same ground pooled and plain match (0.19% of pixels over
  8/255, on lines); 6,400 clusters (819k triangles) render in 0.75 ms on the CPU and 0.77 ms on the GPU,
  against 3.1 and 1.17 ms as plain meshes, draw lists 0.3 against 0.85 ms; 3 KB a cluster against 36 KB.

# Hadley Orbital Works trades at its dock

- **Works stand on a site** (`economy::Site`): a land office's ground, or a rig, which has no
  ground, parcels or zoning. A rig is a place with no people whose market is its own works
  (`Economy::add_rig`, from `world::rigs`' bodies at the start).
- **Its owner trades there** (`Place::trader`): a pilot docked on Hadley's top buys from and sells
  to Hadley (`org.hadley`, a company of the land office's), not an exchange.
- **It runs on what it holds:** its record gives it no starting stock, so it stands at 0% until
  someone brings deuterium for its fusion station and ore, lime, electrodes and alloying metals for
  its furnace. It bids for each (nickel-iron ore 255 cr/t, deuterium 153).
- `Economy::owner(works)`: a works' owner, its parcel's or its rig's, used by running, setting up,
  the company operator and the panel. Each works keeps how it ran (`Works::last`); the economy
  panel lists a place's works from there.
- The hypernet has no node at a rig: the panel reads "no word". You learn its prices by going there.
- Test: `a_rig_is_its_owners_market`. Example `rig_dock`: set down on its top by hand and read
  its quotes. Dev: `UNIVERSE_PLACE` picks the economy scenario's place.

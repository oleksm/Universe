# 2026-09-30 — Re-architecture R4a: the law, as a service, from the event log

User: "yes" (carry on with R4).

## `universe-services`, and the law

- **New crate `universe-services`**: the world's institutions. It depends only on the contracts
  (`universe-protocol`).
- **`law::Law` owns who is fair game:**
  - each ship's standing (until when), and every ruling (the last 1,000);
  - each ruling carries its **cause** (`Cause::Event { tick, index }`: the hit's place in the
    tick's event log) and its **evidence** (shooter, target, time);
  - firing on a ship that isn't fair game makes the shooter fair game for 10 minutes; shooting
    the aggressed is no crime; turrets answer to no one;
  - `declare` sets a standing (scenarios, tests) with its own cause; `forget` clears the record
    of a ship that's gone.

## Out of the core

- `Ship::aggressed_until` and `Ship::aggressed` are gone. The weapons code only lands hits; who's
  to blame is no longer its business.
- `ShipEvent::Hit` says whether it was weapon fire (`weapon`), so a collision is never taken
  for aggression.
- Turrets get each armed ship's standing from the law (`Armed::aggressed`).

## In the tick

- **The tick's event log** (`Universe::log`) holds the combat phase's events, in order: what
  causes point into.
- **The law rules on each weapon hit in log order,** so a ship just made fair game is fair game
  for the hits after it in the same tick. A ruling that's news goes to the shooter's feed
  (`ShipEvent::Aggressed`).
- **Standings are read from the law** by the frame's snapshot (radar pictures, posses), radar
  contacts, and the view (`View::aggressed_until`, `CraftView::aggressed`). A new ship has a
  clean record.

# PGS1 roots: engine acceptance

- `worlds::pgs` reads canonical PGS1 v1 root terrain and water domains, validates
  section checksums/layout and closed topology, and evaluates the stored f64
  surface with deterministic boundary ownership and shore water queries.
- `Terrain::from_surface` shares that field between physics and drawing queries.
  Unknown water remains explicit and does not fill negative heights with ocean;
  categories remain unknown. Production installation is separate.
- Vendored lab goldens cover root, split and shore cases. Tests also cover
  malformed input, unsupported sections/capabilities, analytical split invariance
  and the terrain adapter. `pgs_accept` checks external S13/S15 manifests and
  queries, measures load/query costs and writes a development height preview.
- Reproduction and integration limits: `docs/pgs1-integration.md`.

- Explicit in-game dev preview: `UNIVERSE_PGS1=<export directory>` with a body and
  planet/lowflight scenario. An instance-level source map is shared by world and
  charts, so collision and rendered heights agree; checked by a contact test.
- Canonical terrain-only shading uses measured-elevation grayscale, preserves negative heights, suppresses
  procedural detail and old bake/line overlays, and shades local mesh slopes.
  Unsupported water-domain previews, player-build previews and seed-only
  saves/recordings are refused. No registered world is replaced.

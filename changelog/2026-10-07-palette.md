  (`panel(alpha)`), in place of copies in 18 files (the HUD's own name for the text, `HUD`, and the

  panel's `#ffb030`), red `#ff4040` (the zoning panel's was `#ff5040`). Every panel's backing is
  `panel(α)` (its own alpha kept).

# 2026-09-30 — Re-architecture R2: contact rules as data

User: "Yes" (carry on with R2).

What happens when a ship touches a structure, or passes through one, is now data registered by
the structure's owner. The core applies it without knowing what a station, a spaceport or a gate
is.

## The core (`world::rules`)

- **`Part`**: ground, sea, hull, slot, ring, opening. It comes from the kernel's contact feature
  (or a trigger, for an opening).
- **`Rule`**:
  - `Lock`: under a speed limit, the body is held. The pose is either a slot (a fixed point, nose
    and wings aligned within a roll limit) or the ground (where it touched, belly down). What it
    says can depend on where it touched: a port's pads announce the port. Otherwise the body is
    wrecked.
  - `Transit`: under a speed limit, through to the twin structure in another system, keeping its
    motion relative to this one. Otherwise wrecked.
  - `Bounce`: under a speed limit, the integrator bounces the body off during the step.
    Otherwise wrecked.
  - `Wreck`: wrecked, with a cause.
- **`Release`**: how a structure lets go of a locked body once its devices are commanded on.
  `Eject` shoots it out along an axis at a speed, nose out (a docking port's launch). `LiftOff`
  lifts it clear along the vertical (a burn with the nose up, or the lift thrusters belly down).
- **Every firing is told**: `ShipEvent::RuleFired { rule, outcome }` ("… docking port: locked",
  "… gate: too fast: wrecked"…).
- **Rules live with each system** in the world's caches, are frozen with the rest for ships
  stepping side by side, and are applied by `rules::apply` (contact) and `rules::release`
  (letting go).
- **Gone from the core**: `station::contact`, `docks`, `dock`, `bounces`, `launch`;
  `spaceport::touch_down`, `lift_off`; `gate::enter`. The world step no longer branches on what
  kind of structure it touched.

## The owners (`world::structures`, standing in for the services until R4)

Each system's rules are registered when it's first looked at:
- **Stations:** a docking port (slot: 25 m/s, 30° roll; holds the ship in the slot; launch at
  40 m/s along the axis) and a hull that bounces under 15 m/s.
- **Landable worlds:** ground that takes a touchdown under 30 m/s, saying which port when it's
  on one; lift-off; seas that wreck.
- **Gates:** an opening that passes a ship under 300 m/s to the linked system, and a ring that
  wrecks.

## The planner

It predicts arrival with the same rules (`rules::apply` on its copy of the ship), so its
prediction can't drift from what the world does.

# Clouds let the ground through (planet merged)

- The lab's clouds shader: a cloud's optical depth scales with the water its air can hold (format 2
  packages; format 1 as before), and a cloud passes about 1 − R of the light behind it, diffusely, so
  over ice and snow the ground shows through and clouds read bright, not as grey sheets (Hoar's dark
  bands). Read by the clouds' format version (`on`).

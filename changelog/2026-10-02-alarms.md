# Alarms

Alarm notes are their own voice: a saw rounded off a little, hard-edged in time, to cut through
everything else. They run on the wall clock, so time warp doesn't hurry them.

- **Hull:**
  - dropping past 50%: the master caution, two notes, once;
  - under 25%: a klaxon, two whoops every 2.5 s until mended, with HULL CRITICAL blinking on
    the HUD.
- **Low fuel:** under a tenth of the tank while anything's burning, three beeps, again every
  20 s. (The HUD's fuel line already warns.)
- **Proximity:** with the impact warning on and a collision ahead faster than a touchdown
  (8 m/s), beeps from 30 s out, quicker as it nears (time/8 apart, down to near-continuous).
- **Missile tracking us:** a fast two-tone warble.
- The synth can start notes later (`alarm_after`, `Voice::Later`).
- The small thrusters at half their loudness (they're small); lift jets as they were.
- Dev scenario `alarms`; the klaxon and low-fuel beeps added to the offline sound test.

# People in the registry: what they need, and what they can do

*Registry only (`fso`). New kinds the game does not read yet.*

- **A vision and a plan:** what a complete civilization has (`docs/vision-civilization.md`), and
  how each part of it is to be described (`docs/registry-civilization-plan.md`).
- **20 needs,** on four rungs: to stay alive (air, water, food, warmth, shelter, medicine), to live
  together (a home, clothes, power, safety, work, news), to get better (schooling, tools, travel)
  and to matter (art, play, company, travel for its own sake). Each says what a person uses a day
  and whose work meets it.
- **36 trades,** from farmer to judge, each with how it is learned and how long that takes.
- **Two reports:** which needs something described can meet (6 of 20 so far), and the trades.

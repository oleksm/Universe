# Buying ships (S3)

The shipyard sells hulls.

- **Buying** (`Universe::buy_hull`, `buy_hull_as` for any pilot): docked at a station, a hull
  with its stock fit costs its frame's price plus its modules at this station's prices
  (`hull_offer`). The ship flown now goes in trade at 60% of its frame and modules.
  - **Built from the place's stock:** metals for the frame (118 t for a hauler), and each
    module's own materials. A station short of them can't build it: "NOT ENOUGH METALS TO BUILD
    IT (40 OF 118 T)".
  - **Carried over:** the cargo, if the new hold takes it; the fuel, up to the new tank. The new
    ship is undamaged.
  - **Refused, with the reason:** not docked at a station; the hold too small for the cargo; not
    enough credits; the same ship.
- **The shipyard's HULLS page** (TAB switches pages): every hull's dry mass, tank, hold, main
  drive and lift as accelerations, turning, and price here; your own hull starred. For the one
  picked: its slots and stock fit, its lift with a full hold against 1 g, the autopilots it
  runs, and what it costs after your ship's trade-in. ENTER twice buys it (`Command::BuyHull`).
  Seen in a frame.
- Ships are dear against the starting 1,000 CR (a hauler costs about 430,000 after the trade-in).
  That's for the economy's balance pass to look at, once ships are part of it.
- Test: refused undocked; bought at the station, at the price less the trade-in, with the cargo
  carried over and the frame's 118 t of metals taken from the station's stock; the same hull
  again is refused. Dev scenario `shipyardhulls`.

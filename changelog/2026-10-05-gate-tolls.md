# A gate's crossing is paid for

SFO 17: "a ship pays to cross by its mass and the span". Now it does:

- **The toll is the crossing's energy** (Dogma's Tube law: `tube_crossing_energy` at the ship's
  natural time) **at the power price** (40 cr/MWh), paid to the system's administration as the
  ship enters the tube. An MC-07 pays about 330 credits for 3 light years, 870 for 8, 2,200 for 20;
  a lighter ship less, by its mass.
- **No clearance for a ship that can't pay:** "REFUSED - THE CROSSING COSTS 870 CR, YOU HAVE 400".
  Pilots see their credits for this (the pilots' view carries them).
- The gate test checks the toll asked for its crossing.
- **Not built:** holding a tube open. The Tube law asks 3.6 GW to hold a 3 ly tube, 24 GW for
  20 ly: 9 to 60 of the registry's 400 MW fusion stations at each gate. Asked of the registry.
- Crossing faster than the natural time (dearer) has no control yet.

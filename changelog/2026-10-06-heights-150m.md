# The ~150 m heights, read where a bake has them (layer B groundwork)

- `worlds::Heights` reads a third level as the lab's survey format proposes it (layer B of the
  terrain relief plan): `fz150.json` (its level and size, as `fz.json`) and
  `fz150_<face>_<x>_<y>.png`, differences from the 600 m heights in quarter-metres, over mountain
  ground only (no tile: no difference). Added to the 600 m and 5 km heights in `at_detail`, so the
  physics (`Detail::Full`) and the drawn ground (`Loaded`, streamed) take the same ground; the tiles
  share the 600 m tiles' cache and byte budget (`UNIVERSE_TILE_MB`), in slots after them.
- The finest ground patches are about 320 m across with 20 m cells: fine enough to show it.
- Not yet seen on real data: no bake has the level. The lab is asked for a sample (a few tiles over
  one of Heath's ranges, through `UNIVERSE_BAKE_TRD1`); until then a bake without `fz150.json`
  reads as before.
- (The cube tile lookup is now `worlds::locate(d, level)`, shared by both levels.)

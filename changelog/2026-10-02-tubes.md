# Gates and relays are tubes

- **One formula** (Dogma's new `Tube` laws, replacing the throat and the hyper-signal pulses):
  a crossing of `m` kg over a span `S` in time `t` costs `ε·m·S·e^(t_nat/t)`, with
  `t_nat = 0.2 s × (S/1 ly) × (m/1 kg)^(1/3)`. Opening a tube is the same formula by its
  equivalent mass `406 kg × (d/1 m)³`; holding it, its opening over 100 months (1% a month).
- **Each constant from a stated target** (docs/world/hyperspace.md, docs/world/README.md):
  data at 200 ms a light year; a 100 t ship under a minute through a 5 ly gate, a capital ship
  several minutes; a pass an S2 plant-hour; opening a 5 ly gate a year of 100 GW (3.2×10¹⁸ J),
  holding it 12 GW; opening a one-ship tube for one pass 4,000 passes' worth; relays next to free.
- **Gate transits** take the natural crossing time for the ship's mass and the lane's span (was a
  flat 10 s): on today's long lanes (16-90 ly) a 100 t ship takes 2-14 minutes; 5 ly gates
  (the real-density galaxy, to come) bring it under a minute.
- **The hypernet carries data in capsules:** a relay hop in a system about 1.5 s (half the flow's
  3 s settle), a gate's data crossing 0.2 s a light year plus the settle.
- Experiments: `tools/experiments/gate_transit.py`, `tube_holding.py`. The Dogma checks test the
  targets; the physics sheet regenerated.
- A transit counts down in game time (it counted real seconds, from when it was a fixed show), and
  its banner reads in minutes when long ("ARRIVING IN 6 MIN"). The gate test now flies through:
  into the tube for exactly its natural time, out at the twin.

# Sound needs air

- **In flight through an atmosphere, the air rushes over the hull** by its dynamic pressure,
  ½ρv² (the air's density there, the speed against the turning atmosphere). A whisper coming in
  to land, a roar on the way in from orbit, at its loudest at 40 kPa. Brighter the faster.
- **On foot where there's air: wind,** rising and falling in gusts, by the air's density.
  - Other ships' engines carry through it: their thrust over the distance squared, a far low
    roar.
- **On foot in vacuum: nothing from outside** (air under 0.005 kg/m³ carries no sound), only
  your own breathing in the suit, in and out.
- **The small thrusters at half again:** a quarter of where they started.
- The synth's new layers: `set_air`, `set_breath`, `set_distant`; all four added to the offline
  sound test.

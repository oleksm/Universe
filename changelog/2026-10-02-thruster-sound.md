# Thrusters quieter and softer

- **No more sharp swish on every small correction:** a thruster's sound eases in over 60 ms and
  out over 200 ms (was 12 and 50), and only past 6% of its push (was 2%): the flight computer's
  constant small pulses no longer each make a "pssht".
- **Lower and darker:** a low rush through a wide band at 450–700 Hz for the small thrusters, 320 Hz
  for the lift (were 1.5–2.4 kHz and 700 Hz, narrow): less hiss.
- **Quieter:** small thrusters at 40% and lift jets at about 40% of before, the valve's knock
  at a quarter; and many firing at once are no louder than a few (shared by the root of how
  many).

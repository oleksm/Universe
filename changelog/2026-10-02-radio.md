# Radio chatter

- **Voices on the radio:** speech you hear but can't make out.
  - A buzz at the speaker's pitch, shaped into syllables by vowel formants gliding from one to
    the next, consonant hisses between, a pause now and then, the pitch falling through a phrase.
  - Through a radio's narrow band (two high-pass stages and a low-pass: no bass) and a little
    overdrive, static under it, the squelch clicking open and closing with a "kssht".
  - Each speaker keeps their own pace and manner (`voice`).
- **Traffic control talks:**
  - asking for clearance, your call goes out (a lower voice), then the controller answers;
  - likewise for being denied, told to hold, and cancelling;
  - a pad assigned: the controller alone.
- **Others' talk:** within 40 km of a station or a port, powered and seated, now and then a
  pilot or a controller every 14–44 s, quieter. A pilot's call is sometimes answered.
- `Audio::radio`. The radio added to the offline sound test.

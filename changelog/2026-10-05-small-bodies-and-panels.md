# Small bodies from the export; plate parts from panels

Merged `fso` (the user's word), with the two pieces that waited on the game's side:

- **Small bodies are the registry's records of the engine's own:** the dwarf planets, crossing
  asteroids, captured moons, centaurs and comets the engine seeds are written out by its export,
  and the loader reads those kinds (`celestial.rs`). The celestial guard holds every one to its
  record: all pass.
- **Plate parts are welded from formed panels:** the cutting table takes the 15% loss. A yard's
  company now sets every module that can be set to anything (the cutting table and panel former
  too, not only the shop modules) to what the build lacks most, down the whole bill. Trethi Yard
  builds an MC-07 in 24.4 days from its day-0 stock.

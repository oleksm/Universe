# 2026-09-30 — On foot: leave the seat, walk the ship, step out on a planet

User: "we can now exit and enter the ship. I can do it even in space. When I leave the seat, I am
inside my ship and can walk through the cockpit. I can also open the door and walk on a planet
when I land. Then come back and sit in the cockpit."

## World: `crew`

- `Person { place }`, where the place is one of:
  - `Seat`: flying the ship.
  - `Aboard { position, yaw, pitch }`: standing in the ship, in its frame, on magnetic boots (a
    coasting ship is in free fall; the boots hold you to the deck). The ship flies on with its
    devices as last set, the autopilot included.
  - `Outside { body, position, velocity, yaw, pitch }`: on a body's surface, in its rotating
    frame, under its real gravity (μ/r²). Walk 1.6 m/s, run 4.5 m/s, jump at 3 m/s, which comes
    out about 0.35 m high in 1.3 g. You're kept out of the sea.
- **Interior**: three rooms along the ship's axis (a cockpit 6×7 m with the seat, a 2.2 m
  corridor, a cabin 10×8 m), a deck at y = -2.4 in the ship's frame, and 3.2 m of headroom. You
  slide along walls, and doorways join the rooms.
- **Hatch** (the cabin's port wall): it opens only when the ship rests on a body's surface. It
  refuses when docked ("NO GANGWAY YET") and in flight ("NO SPACEWALKS"). The ramp's foot is on
  the ground beside the ship, clear of the wing, and F there boards.
- `WalkCommands` (move, run, jump, look, use) and `CrewEvent` (StoodUp, SatDown,
  SteppedOutside, CameAboard, HatchRefused). `Person::eye` gives the view; `Person::reach` says
  what's usable.
- Tests: `stand_up_walk_the_ship_and_sit_back_down` (walls, hatch refused in flight, seat) and
  `step_out_on_a_planet_walk_jump_and_come_back_aboard` (feet on the terrain, 16 m in 10 s, jump
  height matching v²/2g, boarding again).

## Sim and game

- `Universe::crew`, `walk`, `pilot_eye` and `pilot_reach`. The pilot's feed gets
  `Event::Crew`. A wrecked ship, or one in transit, puts you back in the seat.
- **F** gets up from the seat; the stick and triggers are released, and the ship flies on. On
  foot:
  - WASD walks (SHIFT runs, SPACE jumps);
  - the mouse or arrows look;
  - F uses what's in reach, with a prompt: `F SIT DOWN`, `F OPEN HATCH`, `F BOARD SHIP`.
- **First-person view** at eye height.
  - Aboard: the interior is drawn instead of the hull, with shaded walls, deck and ceiling, the
    doorways, the cockpit's windscreen and canopy open to space, the seat and console, and the
    hatch frame in amber.
  - Outside: the hull, landing legs down to the ground (a landed ship's origin is 12 m up), the
    ramp from the hatch, and a fine 4 m grid underfoot.
- The on-foot HUD shows the room or surface, gravity, the distance to the ship, and the keys. The
  action grid and flight readouts are hidden.
- Dev scenarios: `aboard` (the cabin, in orbit) and `outside` (landed at the pad, stepped out,
  looking back at the ship).

## Not yet

- Spacewalks (EVA) and walking in stations.
- Water: you stop at the shore.
- The rendered ground is a grid that interpolates the terrain, so feet can look a little off on
  rough ground.
- The ship can leave without you if an autopilot is flying it when you step out.

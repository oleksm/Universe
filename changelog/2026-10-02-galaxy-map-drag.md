# Galaxy map: drag, and lane lengths

- **Drag (left button) moves the galaxy map** under the cursor (the wheel zooms, arrows pan, HOME
  centres on you, as before).
- **Each gate lane shows its length** at its middle ("40.9 LY") from the 50 ly zoom (the scale
  bar) inward: today's lanes run 16.5-89.7 ly.

# Contact group: excision, lock closure and rim

`universe-terrain-contact` builds a complete optional contact group from actual resident patches: the band on the
runtime host radius, the crossed resident triangles cut to the band's outer chain and drawn as a rim in the band's
frame, footprint vertices locked with their seam-relation closure, skirts cut, and refusal on any unsupplied
dependency, coverage error, seam over 10 µm or widened patch seam. All six actual snapshots build (CPU). Nothing is
wired into the game yet; defaults, physics and source are unchanged. See docs/diagnostics/contact-group-excision.md.

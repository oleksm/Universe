# Textured, physically based models from glTF (renderer stage 1, first part)

- **glTF 2.0 import** (`engine::PbrModel::load_gltf`, the pure-Rust `gltf` crate: Windows too):
  every mesh of the default scene placed by its node, glTF's metallic-roughness materials (base
  colour, metallic-roughness, normal map, emission), images as RGBA8 with mip chains (colour ones
  filtered in linear light); tangents from the file or worked out from the UVs.
- **A PBR pipeline** in the scene pass (`pbr.rs`, `shaders/pbr.wgsl`): GGX microfacets with
  Smith shadowing and Schlick Fresnel over a Lambert body; normal maps; lit as meshes are (the
  sun with its shadows, the reflecting planet's light, the ambient share); emission into the
  HDR scene (it blooms); models cast and take shadows (`pbr_shadow.wgsl`).
- `Frame::model_pbr(&model, &transform)`.
- **A Blender test hull** built headless: `blender -b -P tools/blender/test_hull.py -- out.glb`
  (a chamfered octagonal body, cockpit, pods, nozzles; numpy-made textures: painted plates and
  seams, hazard stripes, grime, a normal map, paint vs bare metal, lamps) →
  `assets/models/test_hull.glb`.
- Dev: `UNIVERSE_MODEL=file.glb` shows a model ahead of the eye (`UNIVERSE_MODEL_AT` metres,
  `UNIVERSE_MODEL_TURN` radians, `UNIVERSE_MODEL_CLEAN` no HUD or own ship); scenario `showcase`
  (the sun over the eye's shoulder).
- Note: the scene already renders at full window resolution with 4× MSAA; the 540 is only the
  HUD's layout grid.

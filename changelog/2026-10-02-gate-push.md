# A gate's transit as a push through space

Before, a transit showed the system left behind with amber and cyan rings flying at the eye, then
popped into the next. Now (`transit_push`):

- **The eye rides the line between the two stars:** easing up to speed and down again over the
  transit, turned so the destination is dead ahead of the nose. Every star of the galaxy is seen from
  there; the near ones streak as they sweep across the sky.
- **The two suns:** glows sized by the light reaching us. The one left behind dwindles, the one ahead
  swells.
- **The push:** streaks of light flowing past round the eye along the way, long and bright at the
  middle, still at the ends.
- **Our ship rides it** (chase view), its drive full.
- **A flash** leaving one gate and coming out of its twin.
- **The HUD between the stars:** no system markers, radar, sun glare or instruments; just the transit
  line and its countdown.
- The `transit` scenario takes `UNIVERSE_LEFT` (seconds of transit left when it stops).

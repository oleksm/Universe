# Action buttons only where they apply

- **A button shows only when it makes sense here and for this ship** (greyed: it applies, just
  not now, e.g. a full tank, a sound hull):
  - docked or landed: FUEL UP only at a market; OVERHAUL only at a station; PASSENGERS only with a
    cabin aboard; AUTOPILOT only with a route set; ENLIST/LEAVE only at a held station; FOUND only
    at a station and sworn to no one;
  - in flight: HYPERDRIVE only with one fitted; CLAIM only sworn to a faction in an unclaimed
    system; in combat, GUN and PULSE LASER only with each fitted;
  - the top bar: COMBAT only with a weapon, MINING only with a mining rig.

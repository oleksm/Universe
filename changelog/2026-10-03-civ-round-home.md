# The settled neighbourhood round home

- The four settled neighbours are now one toward each side of home (seen from above, within 8 ly,
  near its plane), not just its four nearest stars, which could bunch on one side.
- Home is the hub: a gate straight to each neighbour, plus loops between the two closest pairs
  side by side (never across home). The stars themselves are unchanged.
- The hub's longest gate (home to Moryemzai, 7.0 ly) is dropped: that neighbour is reached
  through a loop (via Biraidim), so its traffic and news chain through the next system.

# 2026-09-30 — Performance stats on the HUD

User: "print some more perf stats on the top right - engine tick processing time, and whats
useful". The top right shows, all smoothed (an EMA over about 20 frames):

```
219 FPS  4.2 MS                       frame rate, frame time
SIM 1.25 MS  1001 SHIPS  1.3 US EACH  world tick (every ship's turn), per ship
SIM CAN'T KEEP UP                     (red, only when the tick can't reach the time scale)
PLAN 1.1 MS EVERY 0.1 S               flight planner build cost and rebuild interval (when planning)
UPD 1.5 DRAW 1.0 GPU 0.4 IDLE 1.5     where the frame went
13K LINES 57K TRIS 40K PTS            what was drawn
```

The frame breakdown:
- UPD: `Game::update`, which includes the sim.
- DRAW: `Game::draw`, building the draw lists.
- GPU: upload and submit.
- IDLE: waiting for the next swapchain image, which with vsync is time to spare.

- Engine: `Perf` in `Context` (`ctx.perf`). The run loop times update/draw/render and the
  renderer times its wait for `get_current_texture`; `Frame::counts()` gives the draw-list sizes.
- Game: `App::sim_ms` times `step_world`, and the planner's cost was already measured.

Seen in the autodock scenario with 1,000 settlers: the sim takes 1.25 ms for 1,001 ships
(1.3 µs each), and the frame is 4.2 ms with vsync off in screenshot runs.

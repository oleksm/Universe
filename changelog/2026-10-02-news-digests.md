# News outlets and digests

- **An outlet at each settled system's station** (`newsroom`): named for its system and a
  masthead (REMEIPEI DISPATCH, DISTIK WIRE...). It hears what reaches its station over the
  hypernet, as anyone there would, and every 10 minutes puts out a digest of what it heard since
  the last: its own system first (ships destroyed and by whom, trades and turnover, the busiest
  market, the biggest deal), then "FROM <SYSTEM>: ..." for each other system it heard from.
- **A digest is a broadcast** from its station, carried over the net like any news: your own
  system's in seconds, a neighbour's after the gates, none while you're off the net.
- **NEWS (F11):** the digests heard, newest first, with where each is from, how long ago it went
  out and how long it was on its way. **The ticker**, right side over the kills: the latest digest
  as it reaches you, its outlet and lead, for 20 seconds. (Every letter is taken in flight, so
  NEWS is a fixed key, like F7.)
- The outlets are clients of the world, like traders: what's news is theirs to say.
- Dev scenarios `newsdesk`, `newsticker`.

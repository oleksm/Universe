# Sea ice is ice, not open sea

- The sea's look (the lab's `sea_material`) was laid wherever the ground was below sea level, on
  every baked world: it tested the calm map's alpha, and the bakes' `globe_spec.png` is one channel,
  read as alpha 1 everywhere. The map is the lab's open water times its calm (0 on land and on sea
  ice, 0.35..1 on open water), so the test is now its value (`calm.r > 0.1`).
- Hoar (Treistun e, the home world, TRE1) is frozen over: its whole sea is ice in its bake, and was
  drawn as blue water with the lab's clouds over it. It now shows its ice, as the lab's viewer does.
  (Its cloud layer still shades the ice in dark bands: the lab's to look at.)

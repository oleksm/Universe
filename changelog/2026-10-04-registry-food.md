# Food in the registry

*Registry only (`fso`). The game reads the new goods, modules and companies; nothing is placed yet.*

- **57 foods:** grains, roots, pulses, oilseeds, sugar crops, ten vegetables, ten fruits and nuts,
  coffee, tea and cocoa, meat, fish, milk, eggs, and what is made of them (flour, bread, oils,
  sugar, cheese, butter, beer, wine). With hay, feed, the animals alive, and what is given off
  (straw, manure, bran, whey...), 75 goods in all.
- **18 kinds of farm and food works:** the field, the market garden, the orchard, the grow hall
  under lamps, the livestock barn, the fish farm, the mills and presses, the dairy, the meat works,
  the bakery, brewery and winery, the fertiliser works, the silo and the cold store. 76 recipes,
  each balanced: a crop is made of carbon dioxide and water and gives off oxygen.
- **9 food companies,** invented: growers on Treistun d and e, orchards, millers, a dairy,
  stockmen, a grow-hall company, a fertiliser maker, brewers and bakers.
- **The sum:** feeding the system's 220,000 people a full diet takes about 350 km2 of farms,
  nearly half of it to feed animals. Written up in `docs/registry-food.md`.

# Every station has SAMs

- **Every station is defended:** 3–4 SAM turrets round the platform, in its deck's plane, clear
  of it. Before, 70% of stations had 2–4, so some stations, the home one included, stood
  undefended while pirates worked their approach.
- **A station's turrets reach 20 km,** like a spaceport's (they were 6 km): the approach, the
  holding ring above the deck, and the run-in. Pirates keep out of turret reach (as before), so
  they now hunt beyond it.
- Gates and spaceports are as before (half of each defended).
- Test: every station in the gate network has at least 3 turrets, all reaching 20 km.

# More hulls; NPCs fly their own (T5)

Four hulls join the Drover, each with its own shape, slots, stock fit and price, and its
character from the physics:

| Hull | Dry | Hold | Main | Lift (empty / full hold) | Turns (rad/s², pitch/yaw/roll) | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drover | 60 t | 20 t | 30 m/s² | 25 / 20 | 1.4 / 1.3 / 3.5 | 100,000 |
| Sprint courier | 31 t | 4 t | 39 | 19 / 18 | 1.9 / 2.0 / 4.5 | 60,000 |
| Bulk hauler | 175 t | 150 t | 31 | 25 / 16 | 0.7 / 0.6 / 1.6 | 240,000 |
| Prospector | 80 t | 40 t (two racks) | 25 | 21 / 15 | 1.1 / 1.0 / 1.9 | 110,000 |
| Interceptor | 38 t | 4 t | 59 | 19 / 18 | 2.7 / 3.8 / 2.8 | 120,000 |

- **Built from new module sizes:** small and large plants, drives, thruster quads, lift, tanks
  (8 t to 80 t), racks (4 t to 150 t), hyperdrives and life support, plus a combat flight
  computer that lets a ship turn faster.
- **Hulls have a price** (the frame's; what's fitted is priced on top).
- **Balanced layouts:** a shape may give its nodes about its centre of mass (`about_centre`), so
  the new hulls' thruster layouts are generated symmetric about it. Every hull gets over 90% of
  its thrust in every direction without turning (tested), and every hull hovers at 1 g with an
  empty hold.
- **NPCs fly hulls for their work** (player parity): pirates interceptors, miners prospectors,
  traders haulers or Drovers, settlers couriers or Drovers. A ship registers with its hull and
  starts with a full tank of its own size. Starting pads range over the 16.
- **Each hull is drawn from its own shape** (seen in a frame: four distinct silhouettes in a row).
- **What's fitted counts:** a test's miner now flies a prospector, because a courier has no
  mining rig to anchor with.
- Tests: the hauler (heaviest) and the interceptor (nimblest) land from orbit and dock on the
  autopilots. Dev scenario `fleet`.

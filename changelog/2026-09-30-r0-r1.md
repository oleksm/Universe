# 2026-09-30 — Re-architecture R0 and R1: the contract crate; pilots out of the physics

User: "Ok go for it" (the plan in `docs/rearchitecture.md`, decisions accepted as proposed).

## R0 — `universe-protocol`

- **The actuation contract** (client → core): `ShipCommands`, `Controls`, `Triggers`,
  `HyperdriveCommand`, `Destination`. It moved out of the world, which re-exports it so
  existing paths stand.
- **Ticks, ids and stamps:** `Tick`, `BodyId`, `ClientId`, `MessageId`, `Stamped<T>`.
- **`Cause`:** what an authoritative change came from (the rules, a core event, a message).

## R1 — nothing runs inside the physics step but physics

- **`FlightComputer` is gone.** The world's ship step no longer calls into pilot software
  between integration substeps.
  - **`Avionics::fly`**, run once per tick in `prepare` before the step, gives the autopilots'
    say: in hyperdrive, the navigation (orders and steering); otherwise the
    dock/land/gate autopilot. The devices hold its settings through the step.
  - **Hyperdrive orders** are held ship state (`Ship::hyper_orders`), set by commands like any
    device. Dropping out happens when ordered, at the start of a tick.
  - **`Devices`** (the kernel's force callback) only pushes with the thrust it's set to.
- **Turning is physics.** In normal flight the commanded turn rates act over the game time the
  step covers, as everything else does. (In hyperdrive, over real time, as before.)
- **Ticks of at most 1/60 game second.** `Universe::step_world` splits a warped frame into ticks
  (up to 8; beyond that, ticks stretch and the step reports it can't keep up). Pilots' control
  rate stays in game time whatever the warp. The autopilot now commands at 60 Hz (it was 20 Hz
  inside substeps).
- **The flight planner** simulates the autopilot the same way: command, then integrate, with a
  0.05 s control step near the goal. Prediction against the real flight: docking 150.0 s
  planned vs 149.9 s flown; landing 308.9 s vs 309.0 s.
- **Command delay** (`Universe::command_delay`, a test setting for now):
  - a craft's commands, its stick included, reach the devices that many ticks after they were
    given (`vessel::Inbox`);
  - meanwhile its pilot sees its own last settings, so it doesn't fight stale readings.

  The interaction bench runs at 2 ticks (33 ms), the lag pilots will have once they run apart
  from the world (R6).

## Exit test

- Every interaction test passes with a 2-tick command delay: gate turns, pads full then freed,
  docking and launch sharing the corridor, turrets, posses, follow and orbit, air.
- `a_crafts_commands_reach_its_ship_two_ticks_late` proves the delay is real.
- All world, avionics and flight tests pass.

  predates R1. It will be revisited when traffic control becomes a service (R4).

# Clouds by their format

- The clouds' uniform's `on` is their package's format version (`clouds.json` `format`,
  `planet-sim-clouds/N`; 1 when unsaid), not 1: the lab's ask. Format 2's air map holds the water
  the air can carry in its A channel (format 1: the jet's wind), and the lab's shader reads it by
  the version. Format 1 packages draw as before.

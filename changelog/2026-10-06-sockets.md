# 2026-10-06: sockets

- **Attachment on every mount (SFO 19):** points, pattern, and the load each point takes; a frame
  is built to them. 40 mounts.
- **Landing gear is a product:** slot kind `gear`, three legs with parts and chains, three mounts;
  the MC-07 fits four. The engine treats gear as not made yet (legs still from hull parts).
- Engine: a gear slot is dropped from a hull's game slots; `landing_gear` answers `None`.
- **Member catalogue (SFO 13):** 62 new round tubes in 4340, 6061, Ti-6-4 and carbon composite,
  60–300 mm by 1.5–10 mm wall; extrusion press and filament winder modules; lines at the Trethi
  mill. Carbon composite gains stiffness and strength (review). A "Members" report: kg/m, yield
  load, Euler length at 50 and 200 kN, specific strength.
- **Ore bays in ten sizes** (5 to 300 t): hoppers with top doors and a discharge gate, in cargo slots,
  volume at 2,000 kg/m3 of broken rock, mass a tenth of capacity; parts and chains; function
  `ore_bay` (engine: not made yet, the bay is still the hull's).
- **`heat_to_hull`** on drives, lifts and thrusters (a millionth today; the physics stated); a
  **Budgets** report per hull: power, heat against radiators, air and water days.
- **The MC-07 fitted for heat and people:** four radiators (54 MW), four coolant loops (50 MW), a
  six-seat cabin, an air store and a water tank; the Budgets rule counts everything drawn as heat
  aboard; life support's `water_recovery` and `air_recovery`.

# Moons on orbits that last

- **Tides round a close moon's orbit:** as the seed makes a moon, its eccentricity is held to no
  more than heats it past the most active moon known (twice the registry's volcanic threshold,
  2 W/m², about Io's). The draws are as before, so every other body is the same.
- **Treistun's moons are possible now.** The four that were heated past anything known (i I at
  1,866 W/m², j I 531, h I 45, i II 44) and j II are Io-like: volcanic, at 2 W/m². i I averages
  about −180 °C, not 153. Seven moons of rocky planets were stretched past that too and are
  rounder.
- **Tidal heat warms every moon**, a rocky planet's as well as a giant's: tides don't ask what the
  planet is.
- **The charted world's records are written again from the seed** (`celestial_export.py`): 12
  moons' eccentricities and worked-out temperatures. Their stories and descriptions are kept.

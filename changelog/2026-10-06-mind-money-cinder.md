# 2026-10-06: Mind, Money, and Cinder's economy

- **Mind:** the school and the college say what they train (`trains`: every trade learned at each,
  with places), so a settlement's training pipeline is places over the trade's time.
- **Money:** `banking` on an organisation (what it lends against, interest and longest term as
  shares and seconds, advance, reserve) and Nacaubun Savings, the system's bank; `exchange` on the
  Treistun Exchange: only stock is listed, a sale settles in the warehouse's record, a half percent.
- **Cinder's economy:** Ashfall Camp, a sealed shaft mine on the melt-sheet nickel body
  TRB1-INI-000256A-06 (84 Mt at 1.47% Ni, 1.72% Cu, 2.5 km down) with its concentrator, power, life
  support and yard; the concentrator takes impact-melt ore; a nickel smelter at Port Zaudalein,
  whose sulphur is the source Eikir's chemical works lacked.

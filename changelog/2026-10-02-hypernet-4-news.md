# Hypernet 4: news at the speed it can travel; the net as a tree and a map layer

- **The net routes as a tree:** each relay keeps one uplink, to the neighbour that gets it to the
  backbone soonest; messages hop relay to relay. When a world turns or moves a relay's line shut,
  it switches to another neighbour. (Before, the map drew every pair in reach: a mesh nobody
  would pay for.)
- **NETWORK is a layer on the map** (K), not another map: over the usual chart, each relay's uplink
  coloured by lag, dark relays red, a ring round each asteroid field by whether your comm would be
  on the net there, your line in; over the usual list, a NET column (lag, or DARK) and your status.
  The list has a header row now.
- **Capture and delivery:** kills carry where they happened, trades their market. A kill is known
  if our own comm hears it or a relay on the net does (in its capture range, the line clear); a
  trade once its market's relay is on the net. From its system's backbone through gate relays to
  ours, and out to us while we're on the net. Off the net nothing new comes; back on, it all does.
- **The feeds show what we've heard,** from when we heard it: "- IN STURAIE (13 S AGO)" for news
  from another system or that was long on the way. A trade at a port 5 AU out turns up 42 minutes
  later; a fight out in a dark belt never does.
- One fast test: a kill by a relay heard at once, one unseen never, one next door after the gates.
  (79 tests.)

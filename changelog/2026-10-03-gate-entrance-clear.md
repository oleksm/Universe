# Gate traffic control keeps the entrance clear of traffic coming out

- A ship out of a gate's tube comes out on the ring's entry side heading back down the run-in, so
  traffic control now holds the corridor (the next ship waits on the waiting ring, off the axis)
  while traffic is coming out: a ship due out of that tube within 60 s (about a final run's
  length), or out and heading away down the run-in till it's clear of it (4.5 km along the axis,
  half a ring's radius aside). Not the whole tube: only the entrance.
- Checked through the sim: due out, held; out and heading away, held; clear of the run-in, given.
- The spawn and the game's start no longer chirp (440→880 Hz, 440→660 Hz): a new ship's frame
  settling (a low swell, a thud) and its systems spooling up; at start, the spool.

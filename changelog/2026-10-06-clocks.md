# The engine on its clocks (first step of the migration)

- The registry's clocks (`standards/Engine`, the tick tree the user settled) are generated into
  the engine: `ClockKey`, one variant a clock record, and `ClockHandler`, one method a clock
  (`registry/build.rs` from `Engine/metadata/scheduling`). `sim::clocks::Binding` binds every
  one, so a clock added to or taken from the registry doesn't build until it's bound; its test
  holds every period to a whole number of realtime ticks.
- Periods are read from the records and nowhere else (`registry::clock_every`):
  - the tick: `clock.realtime`, 0.005 s, so the sim runs at 200 Hz (was 60). Measured: 1.85 ms
    a tick with 1,000 ships in low flight over Heath, 1.7 docked (60 Hz: 1.8): the same per
    tick, 3.3 times a second; inside its 5 ms.
  - the economy: `clock.economy`, 10 s (was 600). Its test steps an hour at that period and waits
    for a whole ingot in the warehouse (the works here draw them out one at a time).
  - the markets' boards: `clock.market`, 1 s (was 120).
- Not yet moved, no clock owns them in the records: standings (5 s), the dead-man rule (30 s),
  the land levy (a day), news, the recorder. Asked of the Scientist. Rails, the NPC lane,
  machinery and bubbles are bound but not built: every craft is still on the realtime clock.
- `planet` merged: the clouds' facets over the sea gone (the lab's f32 fix), the cloud amount,
  rotated octaves, the relief fading in, the cloud cache's read side (not yet plumbed).
- The Scientist's placements (fso 450e6adf): a twelfth clock, `clock.administration` (per
  settlement, a day): the build refused it until bound, as meant. The land levy falls due at its
  period; standings are taken in at the market's step (1 s; their own 5 s timer gone); the
  dead-man rule is looked at each machinery step (1 s; it was every 60 ticks, which at 200 Hz
  would have been 0.3 s). The recorder's 0.25 s is 50 realtime ticks already; news on the client
  is the client's.
- The administration's clock is every 10 s by the user's word (fso 6aa2c91e): the land levy
  accrues and is charged each step at its rate a second (the code already took its period from
  the clock; only its comment said a day).

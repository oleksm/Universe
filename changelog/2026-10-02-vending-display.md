# The vending machine shows what it sells

- **Its window is a display:** dark glass, lit, two shelves with what it sells (a red cola can, a
  blue water bottle, a yellow crisp bag; a chocolate bar, a protein bar), read left to right as you
  face it; the one picked on its panel outlined.
- **The panel** has each item's icon beside its name, and sits to the right of the machine (in
  sight) and clear of the messages, which ran over it.

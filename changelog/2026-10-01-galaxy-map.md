# Galaxy map

- **U** from the navigation map opens the galaxy: all 40,000 stars from above, in their
  classes' colours; the systems we've been to bright (named when zoomed in enough to tell them
  apart); the settled gate network's links; where we are; a light-year scale bar; and the
  count — stars, explored (and what share of the galaxy that is), settled. Wheel or W/S zooms
  (from the whole 19,000-light-year disc down to a few light years), arrows pan, HOME comes
  back to us, U/M/ESC close (M back to the system map).
- The systems we've been to are remembered, and kept in the quicksave.
- Lists repeat when the arrow is held (the navigation map's targets, the market's goods):
  one step, a short pause, then about fifteen a second.

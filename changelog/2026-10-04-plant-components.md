# What every industrial module is built of

- Each of the 31 industrial modules (SFO 10) now lists its major components: 127 kinds (a hot rolling
  mill's roughing stand, finishing stands, rolls, run-out table, coilers and drive motors; a fusion
  power station's reactor vessel, magnet coils, shielding, generator sets, cooling plant and
  transformers), each with a description, how many, and its weight.
- They are parts, filed under the module (`standards/SFO/metadata/parts/<module>/`), shown on the
  module's page, and added up to the module's weight.
- A Plant report: each module's components, weight, weight over its floor, and how many of its
  components say what they are made from (none yet: the next layer).
- The weights and counts are guesses from memory, each marked to review, until published figures
  replace them.

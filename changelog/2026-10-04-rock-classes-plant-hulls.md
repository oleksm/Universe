# Rock classes, what plant is made from, six hulls compared

- **Rock classes** in the celestial registry: the game's four kinds of asteroid as records (density,
  albedo, composition, where they form, what they yield), held to the code by the celestial test.
- **What plant components are made from**: 101 of the 127 taken as structural steel plate or forged
  steel (guesses, to review); A36 plate as mill stock, rolled on the rig. Each module's page and the
  Plant report give the stock it takes.
- **Hulls**: the game's five stock hulls recorded beside the MC-07 (class, maker, volume, frame, fuel,
  hold, fit, thrust), with 23 more equipment records for what they fit. A Hulls report compares all
  six: weight bare and loaded, what each carries, the space its equipment takes, drive and lift, and
  how many loads of each hold the rig's daily rock and a gate ring are.
- **SFO 18, Hauler**: a hauler defined by its job. Hulls are now filed under SFO 2, Spaceships.

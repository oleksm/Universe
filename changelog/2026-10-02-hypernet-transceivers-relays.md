# Hypernet: transceivers and hyper relays

- **Two products, in space only** (nothing of it works from a world's ground):
  - **Transceivers** (Tallis STATION and BEACON TRANSCEIVER, 0.5 AU): the towers. Ships' comms
    connect to one within its radius, at light speed; they hear what happens round them.
  - **Hyper relays** (Halcyon HYPER RELAY, new): link two sites through hyperspace, at Dogma's
    hyper-signal limit (slower near worlds), so news crosses a system in seconds, not hours.
  - Ports and outposts carry a **ground terminal** up to the transceivers over their world.
- **The system's net:** the station, the gates, an **orbital site** round every planet and moon
  (TALLIS ORBITAL SITE: a transceiver and a hyper relay), and claim beacons. Each day the relays
  link them the shortest way all told (a minimum spanning tree over the sites as they stand at
  the day's start). Lag over a link: the hyper-signal's time along it.
- **In the home system:** every port on the net, 0.1 s to 45 s; the tree runs world to nearest
  world (D to B, C, E, G; D to F, H). Asteroid fields between the worlds' radii are off the net.
- **The map:** each site's transceiver radius drawn to scale; lines: the relay tree, and each
  port up to its transceiver.
- A claim beacon is now a transceiver and a hyper relay (both paid for, with the fee).

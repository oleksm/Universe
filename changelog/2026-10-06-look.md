# A punchier camera

- The user's choice (the lab's proposal): exposure 0.5 → 0.9 and a look after the AgX curve
  (contrast: a power of 1.35; colour: 1.4 times the distance from grey), for the whole picture.
  Planets read richer (ocean, desert, whiter clouds); ships and stations change a little (more
  contrast, paint a touch richer). Frames: the lab's `out/look_before_after.png`, and
  `out/from-integrator/look_ships_before_after.png`. The lab's air fix (its tables at ground
  albedo 0.12, ~40% less bright from orbit) comes with the next surface versions.

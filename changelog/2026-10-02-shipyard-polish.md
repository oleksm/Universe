# Shipyard polish

- **A turning wireframe:** the hull turns slowly, seen from a little above, its back faces
  hidden, with its round drive outlines, thrusters, mounts and centre of mass. It's on the HULLS
  page (beside the view from above), the PLAN page and the DESIGN page.
- **Your designs are marked** "(YOURS)" in the hull list.
- **Every page has its keys along the bottom.**
- **Module lists are sorted** smallest first, then cheapest, with each module's size shown.

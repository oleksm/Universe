# The MC-07 painted, not burned: the bake's UV layout fixed

- The first baked MC-07 looked burned (nearly black). Its colour atlas was almost empty: the
  hull's UV islands came out as specks (the biggest panel 0.08% of the map, all of them 0.3%),
  so the hull sampled the black between them.
- Cause: `export_hull.py` packed the islands again after smart projection. On a hull this
  detailed (tens of thousands of islands) Blender's packer shrank them to nothing (with a
  margin added instead, to nothing at all). Smart projection already packs every mesh's islands
  together: the extra packing is gone, its margin is two texels (22.5% of the 4096 px map now
  covered, about 75 times as many texels on the hull).
- The materials' own Ambient Occlusion grime nodes are left out of the baked colour (the
  occlusion map carries it, once).
- `assets/models/mc07.glb` re-exported: grey and white panels, orange trim, decals legible.

# The whole galaxy, one density

- **One density field for the whole galaxy:** a disc, two arms and a bulge, scaled to the real
  density near the Sun at home. Its stars come from the seed in 100 ly sectors on demand; the
  charted region is the sectors round home (about 27,000 stars), the same stars the map shows.
- **The galaxy map zooms continuously** from the whole spiral to single stars anywhere: stars from
  the sectors in view (a 40 ly slab round your plane, thinned alike past 30,000 a frame so the
  arms still show), the glow from the same density summed through the disc. No more painted
  spiral round a square of stars: home sits in the glowing outer disc between two arms.
- Stars over the glow fade by opacity (they showed as dark specks).

# The MC-07's spec, balanced on its own terms

The registry's record of the MC-07 is the spec the game is to follow once it is called solid. Changed
there, not in the game:

- **Its weight is its parts'** (154 t), not the frame mass the game works out from its size (88 t).
- **Its hold is its ore bay**, under its two top doors: 669 m3 (the doors' opening as modelled; its
  depth a guess to review), rated for 1,200 t of stony ore. The 4 t cargo racks are off its fit.
- **It lands empty.** Legs, lift and the settlements it can set down at are for the ship without
  cargo (170 t, ground up to 0.79 g). Full (1,370 t) it unloads at a station or outpost in orbit.
- **The main drive's push is checked through the hull**: six sections from the engine forward, each
  for strength and for how closely its skin must be stiffened. Three 2 mm sections (rear pod,
  connector, main hull) would buckle unless stiffened every 11 to 14 cm: left as gaps.
- **Open questions** are kept on the hull's record and shown on its page.

# The MC-07: a hull designed in Blender, flying; an export path for any .blend

- **The MC-07** (the user's design: Deep Core Mining Co., 74 m, hollowed interior for rooms
  to come) is in the game and the default ship (`assets/models/mc07.glb`). Parked pose: gear
  down, ramp and ore bay shut, lasers stowed. 104 t; six main nozzles (four bells, two small,
  sharing the drive), six lift jets (12.7 m/s² against Treistun e's 9.6), thruster quads at
  nose and tail; it stands on its four footpads (12.9 m to its centre).
- **`tools/blender/export_hull.py`**: any .blend into the game as it stands, at a frame of its
  rig; lights, cameras and non-rendering helpers (boolean cutters, volumes) left out. The
  conventions it lacks are placed from part names and materials and printed (`*_Pad` → gear,
  `*EngineGlow*` islands → main nozzles, `*Glass*` → cockpit, `*Ramp*` → hatch,
  `*Laser*_Head` → hardpoints, `Hull_*` → collision boxes kept few so mass isn't counted twice,
  lift jets, thruster quads). Empties in the file win. Rerun after every change.
- Dev: `padwatch` (on the spaceport's pad, watching the ship); `watch` and `padwatch` take
  `UNIVERSE_DIST`, `UNIVERSE_PITCH`, `UNIVERSE_YAW`.
- Not carried over: the procedural textures (brick, noise nodes) don't export to glTF; the hull
  shows flat colours until they're baked to images.

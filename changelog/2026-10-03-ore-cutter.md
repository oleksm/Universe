# The ORE CUTTER: a detailed miner scripted in Blender

- `tools/blender/miner.py` → `assets/models/miner.glb` (headless, 2 s): a 58 m working miner in
  hard-surface style: a raked bridge with framed glazing, a chamfered spine in segments with
  recessed seams over a dark core, big ore pods on struts with orange and dark caps, a dorsal
  superstructure with a mast and a dish, a heavy engine block with three bells (and the pods'),
  a ventral keel. Large faces cut into armour plates (raised, recessed or flush, seeded) with dark
  rims; ~400 greebles laid along the ship (housings, vents, pipe runs) kept inside their faces;
  a 2 m plate texture projected at real scale (seams, grime, wear to bare metal, scratches, rivets,
  its normal map); markings as text on raised placards (no shadow ghosts); navigation lights,
  floods. Follows the import conventions: it flies as the ORE CUTTER (67 t dry, 63 m/s² main,
  18.5 m/s² lift, 31 nozzles, class 3, two racks, two hardpoints, a utility slot).
- PBR: glTF alpha masks (cut-outs: decals, grilles).

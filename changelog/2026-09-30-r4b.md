# 2026-09-30 — Re-architecture R4b: the ledger and the market service

## The ledger (`services::ledger`)

- **Double entry:**
  - parties are pilots (by ship id), markets (by system and place) and the world (where starting
    funds come from);
  - assets are credits and goods (by catalog index);
  - every movement is a `transfer` with its tick and **cause**, journalled (the last 10,000).
- **`balanced()`:** every asset sums to zero across all parties, so nothing is made or lost.
- **Limits:** pilots can't overdraw credits or give goods they don't hold. Markets, backed by
  their economies, can go negative.
- **`settle`** sets a holding against the world (starting funds, a loaded save). **`write_off`**
  returns a lost ship's hold to the world.

## The market service (`services::market`, moved out of the world)

- Generation, prices, stock and demand recovery are unchanged.
- **`Markets`** caches markets, keeps the state of those traded with, and carries out orders.
- **An `Order`** carries the pilot, the market, where the ship is docked (a physical fact
  reported by the core), its cargo mass, the item and the units.
- **A trade** is checked (docked there, banned goods, stock or demand, credits, hold capacity),
  then booked: credits one way and goods the other, both with the request's cause.

## Out of the core

- `Ship::hold` is gone. The core keeps only `Ship::cargo` (the mass), set from the ledger's
  holdings after every trade.
- `HOLD_CAPACITY` (a hardware spec) is in `world::ship`. `docked_at` and `facilities` (physical
  fact, and charts) are in `world::traffic`.
- `Universe::credits` and `Craft::credits` are gone. Credits come from the ledger
  (`Universe::credits()`, `hold()`, `craft_credits(i)`). A trader keeps its own reckoning of what
  it paid (`Craft::paid`), which is pilot knowledge.

## In the sim

- `pilot_trade` is the one path for the player's and the traders' trades. Each trade is a
  message: `Cause::Message { sender, id }`.
- Starting credits for the player and each settler are minted from the world's account.
- A lost ship's hold is written off, its cause the logged respawn event.
- The view carries credits and the hold from the ledger. Saves keep both, and loading settles the
  ledger to them.

# Every product has a maker

- **Device specs are the product's:**
  - a reactor's efficiency is its own (Hadley's rugged fusion plants 35%; Aurel Fusion Works' new,
    lighter, dearer plants 50%);
  - a hyperdrive's field efficiency is its own (Kestrel's standard drives 60%; Halcyon Field
    Systems' new, dearer drives 75%);
  - the world sheet's single field efficiency is gone. The shipyard shows each one's figure.
- **Everything is branded**, and content refuses an unbranded product:
  - every module (drives and thrusters Kestrel; computers, sensors, transponders, nav Orbital; life
    support, racks, cabins, tanks Hadley; weapons the new Garrick Arms; mining gear Cormorant);
  - every hull (the new Tolland Yards: Drover, Hauler; the new Vireo Spaceworks: Sprint,
    Interceptor; Cormorant: Prospector). The shipyard shows "DROVER BY TOLLAND YARDS"; your own
    designs are "YOUR OWN DESIGN".
- **Structures are products too** (`content/base/structures.ron`):
  - Halvard Construction's platform station, spaceport and outpost;
  - Halcyon's gate rings, classes I, II and III, each with its span (10, 25, 50 ly).
  - The ring classes' spans moved there from the world sheet.
  - Not yet: structures' geometry and gear from here, and players building them.

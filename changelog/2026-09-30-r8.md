# 2026-09-30 — Re-architecture R8: interest management (and what 100k costs)

User: "I need to step out, continue until all phases are done".

## The load tool

`cargo run -p universe-sim --release --example load -- N [apart] [stress]`: N settlers as the
game spawns them (stops staggered over ten minutes), or with `stress` all leaving at once; 120
ticks, profiled. It's a tool run by hand, not a test.

## Nobody gets the whole world

- **Pilot views carry full ships only for pilots awake this tick.** A pilot going to sleep
  (parked, or coasting between thinks) says so (`Posting::sleep_until`), and its ship is left
  out of the views until then. Any message to it wakes it (`Universe::tell`).
- **The snapshot is taken once a tick** (at its end, doing for the next start), in parallel,
  and shared by reference (`Arc`), never copied per reader.
- **The cockpit's radar reads the snapshot.** Transponders are sent only for the crafts in its
  radar's range, not for every craft.
- **Pilots post only what's new.** Ships hold their turn until changed (`vessel::Inbox`), as
  they hold engine and thrusters. A pilot with nothing new sends only a keep-alive, once a
  game second.
- **Less often, or a slice at a time:**
  - traffic control's look at who's where runs at 10 Hz;
  - the dead-man rule is checked once a second;
  - the flight recorder samples a fifteenth of the ships each tick (incident reports match the
    other ship's nearest sample, carried to the same moment);
  - collision pairs are found for separate star systems side by side, in 250 m cells with a
    fast hash;
  - ships with weapons safe skip the weapons step.

## Numbers (per tick, pilots apart)

| | before R8 | after |
|---|---|---|
| 10k settlers | 24–34 ms | 1.9 ms |
| 10k, all leaving at once | 34 ms | 21 ms |
| 100k settlers | 72 ms | 25 ms |

## Not met

- **100k at 25 ms is about 1.5× the 16.7 ms budget.** What's left is spread over stepping 100k
  ships (already parallel), collisions and per-tick handling of every ship; there's no single
  big item.
- **No per-system core shards yet:** every ship steps side by side on all cores instead.

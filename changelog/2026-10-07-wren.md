# 2026-10-07: small-craft equipment for the personal commuter

- For the user's two-seat VTOL commuter, which the ships session builds: electric ducted fans in
  three sizes (cycle `ducted_fan`, power as thrust to the 1.5, `min_density`), a hydrolox fuel
  cell, a 100 MJ battery, small hydrolox and methalox tanks (0.5 to 3.5 t), nitrogen and oxygen
  bottles, small water tanks, a scrubber cartridge, a cabin air pack with its own `air_store` and
  `water_store`, and light 60 kN legs. The Budgets report counts a pack's stores and keeps a fan's
  full-thrust power out of the steady balance. The physics brief is in the ships note §16.
- Second round, on the ships session's finding that battery rate binds a VTOL: high-rate VTOL
  packs in three sizes (4 kW/kg, 0.5 MJ/kg, `cycles` on the battery kind), kilowatt thermal (a
  10 kW radiator, a 20 kW loop, a 20 kW air cooler for worlds with air), a 180 kN leg.

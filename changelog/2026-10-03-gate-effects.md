# Gate effects, and no network in the tube

- **Inside the tube (the pilot):** the tunnel is back, brushed up: rings of the held medium rush
  past (quickening going in), wavering with its unstable flow, their light drifting between cyan
  and amber down the tube; plasma streaks along the wall; the far end's light grows as the
  crossing ends; a flash going in and coming out; our ship at full burn in chase view.
- **Seen by everyone:** a ship going in flares the ring, and the plasma it pushed shoots out the far
  side along the axis, the way the tube runs: a bright pulse quickening away, a trail of the ring
  narrowing behind it (4 s).
- **No network in the tube:** the signal badge goes OFF, no news is heard, and markets show what we
  knew going in (their age still counts on).

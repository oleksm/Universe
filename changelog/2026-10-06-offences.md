# Offences charged under the law

- **The law charges** (`services::law::Charge`, `Law::charge`, `Law::charges_of`): in a system whose
  administration has a law (`world::order::law`; Treistun so far), an offence it names is charged
  against the ship, with its evidence (the event logged) and who suffered by it. The charges stay on
  the record; a ship's own are those since it was last replaced.
- **What is charged now** (`sim::order::judge`, after the combat phase):
  - **piracy:** opening fire on a ship that isn't fair game (where the law rules on the hit);
  - **murder:** an innocent brought down by one who was fair game;
  - **reckless flying:** a ship wrecked with nobody firing on it (a crash into a world, a
    structure or another ship: both ships in a collision, for now).
- **The insurer reads the charges:** a loss brought on by an offence it refuses (piracy, murder,
  smuggling, reckless flying) gets the basic ship. So a crash on landing is not insured.
- The pilot is told: "CHARGED UNDER TREISTUN'S LAW: PIRACY".
- Not charged yet (nothing in the game does them): theft, smuggling, fraud, evasion; unlicensed
  mining waits for licences (Treistun's law wants one, and none can be had yet); zoning breach
  waits for building outside the zoning to be recorded.
- The combat test checks piracy and murder.

# Ships read their own numbers (S1 groundwork)

No change in behaviour: every ship carries what it's built as (`ship::Class`, so far only the
Cobra), and everything that used the global ship constants reads the ship's own `spec()`
instead — mass, tank, hold, thrusts, turn rates, collision radius, drag, hull strength — in the
physics, damage, heat, mining, autopilots, refuelling, the NPC traders and miners, and the
displays. The market's hold check takes the room left in the hold from the order (a physical
fact the core reports), so the market service needn't know ships. `SHIP_RADIUS` stays as the
size traffic lays out room for (pads, slots, corridors). Old saves load as Cobras.
The cargo view's close key is read from the bindings (it said 4).

# NPC miners work the belt

- **One miner in three works the belt** (the operator's choice, by its seed; invented). Landed at
  its port, it surveys the belt as a player's prospect does, picks one of the best few rocks over
  20 m among the nearest it resolves, lifts off, climbs clear, and follows the rock out on its own
  engines; there it anchors and digs as at a field, and full, flies to the nearest market to sell.
- Only a port inside the belt finds rocks near enough: of Treistun's, Port Trethi (the nearest about
  114,000 km off, an hour or two out). A belt miner anywhere else works a field as before.
- Miners sell where there is a market (a port with an exchange-approved warehouse), the nearest.
- The miner test now also sends a belt miner out from Port Trethi, and puts it by its rock to dig.

# 2026-09-30 — SAM turrets, shelter, the hyperdrive jam

User: "next big thing - SAM turrets. Those can be placed around stations, gates, on the ground
around space ports. It will auto shoot aggressor. Lets make default range 2x of standard ship ones.
Some ports have it some dont. Pirates will try to be smart about it. Everyone else too."

## World

- **`turrets`**: fixed guns generated from the seed (same seed, same defences):
  - stations: 70% chance, 2–4 turrets on a ring around the middle (1.6 × station size);
  - gates: 50% chance, 2–3 on the ring;
  - spaceports: 50% chance, 3–4 on the ground in a 450 m ring around the pads.
- They're fixed to their body's rotating frame, so they move with it.
- Each one fires on the nearest **aggressed** ship within `TURRET_RANGE` = 6 km (twice a ship's
  gun range). The target can be flying or landed, but it needs a clear line of fire: no shooting
  through the station or the ground.
- They lead their shots with the shared kernel `intercept` and fire the same slugs a ship's gun
  does, at 5 rounds/s. They never shoot the innocent.
- Combat ids for turrets start at `1 << 40`. Kills are credited to "SAM TURRET (place)".
- `World::turret_motions` and `World::covered` give the turrets and whether a point is covered.
- **Hyperdrive jam**: any weapons hit jams the hyperdrive for 15 s (`damage::HYPER_JAM`), as if
  the hit disrupts its field. A ship under fire can't just jump away: it fights or runs on its
  engines. Trying to engage shows "HYPERDRIVE JAMMED" (`ShipEvent::HyperdriveJammed`). The
  autopilots wait until the drive is free. Collisions don't jam it.

## Being smart about it

- **Pirates** (`hunter`):
  - they don't pick prey within turret reach plus a 2 km margin;
  - they break off a chase that goes under the guns;
  - `keep_clear` brakes and pushes them out of any turret's reach, the same way they keep clear of
    bodies and other ships.
- **Everyone else** (`combat::flee`):
  - a non-pirate hit in flight gives up its clearance;
  - it then runs for the nearest defended station or spaceport (not gates), which is put next in
    its route;
  - the hyperdrive jam means it has to make the run on its engines.
- Traffic sightings count a ship under turret cover as sheltered.

## Game

- HUD: turrets within 15 km show as triangle markers labelled "SAM". When you're aggressed, their
  range rings show in red.
- The nav map shows a SAM column for defended places.
- Dev scenario `sam`.

# Modules, slots, power (T2)

A ship is a hull and what's fitted in it.

- **Modules** (`modules.ron`, `world::modules`). Each is a real thing: mass, volume, the power it
  draws at work, a price, a size class (1–4), a brand (empty until T4), and what it does:

  | | Kinds |
  |---|---|
  | base blocks | power plant (its output), main drive, thruster quads, tank, flight computer (its turn limits), transponder, sensors (their range), life support |
  | specialised | belly lift, cargo racks, hyperdrive, gun, laser, mining rig, nav computer (the autopilots it runs: docking, landing, gate, follow, hyperdrive, route) |

- **Hulls** are a frame (its mass, shape, drag and strength) with **slots** (a kind and a size
  each), a **stock fit**, and nozzles tied to slots, each at a share of its slot module's
  rating.
- **Everything is derived:**
  - dry mass is the frame plus its modules;
  - tank and hold capacity come from the tanks and racks;
  - each nozzle's thrust is its module's rating times its share;
  - turning limits come from the flight computer;
  - the power budget is the plant's output against the modules' draw;
  - the autopilot features come from the nav computers.
- **Refused at load, with the reason:** a module in the wrong kind of slot or too big for it; a
  base block missing (every ship must carry a plant, drive, thrusters, tank, flight computer,
  transponder, sensors and life support); modules drawing more than the plant makes.
- **The Drover** is a 32.8 t frame with 15 slots and a stock fit weighing 27.2 t, which gives
  exactly its old numbers: 60 t dry, 30 t of fuel, a 20 t hold, the same thrust on every nozzle.
  No change in flight.
- **Not yet used:** what's fitted doesn't yet gate anything (the gun without a hardpoint, an
  autopilot without its nav computer). That comes with outfitting, when fits can differ.

## The guidance banner on top

The banner of an approach's or a follow program's steps, with its ETA, has the very top line
of the screen; the mode bar sits under it while one's up.

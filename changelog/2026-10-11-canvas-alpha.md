Canvas geometry now has explicit opaque, alpha-mask and blended material modes. Within each canvas, opaque/masked triangles write depth before blended triangles draw far-to-near without depth writes. UI is an overlay over the scene; separate canvas scenes retain independent depth. The legacy triangle API infers blend from vertex alpha. This fixes panes hiding later background geometry and adds mask cutoff handling; intersecting transparent triangles remain subject to centroid-sort limits.

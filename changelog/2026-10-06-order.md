# 2026-10-06: order

- **Law on an administration** (`org.*.law`): jurisdiction, offences with penalties (shares and
  terms, never sums), enforcement (constables, judges, patrol craft, response), policies (duty,
  land rate, licences). Dictionary: offence, penalty, jurisdiction. Treistun's code written.
- **Buildings of order:** watch house, courthouse, gaol.
- **The insurer** (`org.treistun-mutual`): what it covers, premium and excess as shares, a
  replacement delivered at a yard, losses it refuses. Organisations may be insurers or banks.
- **The pirates' hold:** a band (`org.shikra-hold`) and its rig in Biraidim, a system with no law.
  An address may be at a rig.
- `docs/registry-order.md`: the rules a mind should follow from this; people are finite.
- **The ways back** (`law.redress`): appeal within 30 days, restitution first, settlement of all but
  murder, surrender halves a term, service at 2:1, a year's expiry on small matters, pardon;
  `recognises` for reciprocal outlawry (Treistun: none).

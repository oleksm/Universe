# Hearth as its run makes it

- Merged fso (the Scientist's `da14368c`): Hearth (Treistun e, the home world) is baked from the
  lab's TRE1: a snowball with no life and no oxygen (nitrogen with 0.35% carbon dioxide), its ice
  over continents and frozen seas. The game reads its bake by its record as any baked world's: its
  ground, colours, air and clouds; Port Nacaubun breathes from its life-support plant. Also: the
  processes retired (recipes are the modules'), the camps' tailings dams and waste dumps.
- **A world's climate from its record** (`climate::mean_temperature`): where the run gives a mean
  surface temperature (Hearth 216.2 K, about −57 °C), it's the world's, and the formula's shape
  (by latitude, day and night) is kept about it (`recorded_offset`). The seed's 264 K is gone.

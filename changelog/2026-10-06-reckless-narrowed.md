# Reckless flying only where others are put at risk

- A wreck with nobody firing on it is reckless flying only into another ship, or within
  `RECKLESS_REACH` (5 km, invented) of a structure (station, gate, rig) or a port's pads
  (`sim::order::near_others`). A wreck out on a world or among the rocks harms only its pilot: no
  charge, no fine, and the insurer pays.
- The combat test checks both.

# Ship equipment is built from parts

*Registry only (`fso`).*

- **Everything the MC-07 carries has parts:** its drive, thrusters, lift, tank, fusion plant,
  hyperdrive, computers, transponder, radar, comm, life support and mining rig. 94 parts, each with
  a weight, what it is made from and the works that makes it.
- **The shares come from real designs** where there is one: NASA's Discovery II fusion engine, the
  space station's life support, Sentinel-1's radar, the Mars orbiter's radio.
- **A report follows each product back to a works.** 71 of 107 lines are complete. The rest wait on
  a titanium, stainless and copper works being placed, and on three materials nothing makes yet.
- A review of all 57 equipment records: `docs/ships/equipment-review.md`.

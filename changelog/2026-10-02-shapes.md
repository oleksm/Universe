# Shapes (C0.5)

- **One geometry per shape, read by everything** (content: `shapes.ron`; `world::shape`):
  - its mesh, from a convex hull of points (from a Blender glTF export later);
  - its solid: exact volume, centre of mass and inertia from the faces (`physics::mesh`), plus
    silhouette by direction, surface area and bounds;
  - detail lines;
  - named nodes: `mount_*`, `nozzle_*` (a direction for the exhaust), `gear_*`, `dock_*`, `cockpit`.
- **Validation:** a shape that isn't a solid, a node named for no role or pointing nowhere, or a
  glTF source (until the first Blender model) is refused at load with the reason.
- **The Drover and the station are data now.** The Drover has its two main nozzles (on
  the rear plate, firing aft) and its cockpit as nodes. Hulls name their shape. The game draws the
  ship and station from their shapes; frames match the old drawing within run-to-run noise.
- **Known gap, for T1:** collision is still the hull's 12 m sphere, though the Drover is 52 m
  across.
- Tests: a cube's and a long box's mass properties are exact; the Drover's shape (52 m across,
  nozzles, cockpit); unsound shapes are refused.

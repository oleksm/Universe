# The HUD organised

- **Status strip** under the mode bar: mode, clock, time rate, system. **Credits** at the top right.
- **Instruments panel** (left) instead of the wall of text: near, altitude, speed (GND near the ground),
  vertical speed; thrust, fuel, hull (and laser, armed) as bars; mass and load, drive, radar count.
- **What's going on** (route, the lock, approach, landed or docked) in a column under the panel.
- **Warnings** in a boxed stack at the top centre: off balance, low fuel, aggressed, missile lock,
  weapons priming, hull critical, skin heat, collision ahead. "Path clear" is no longer shown.
- **Backings:** text over the world sits on a soft dark backing, readable on a bright sky.
- **Debug (F3)** cycles: off, then performance, traffic counters and the trade feed (top right),
  then the profiler as well. Off by default.
- The kill feed shows its last three, backed.

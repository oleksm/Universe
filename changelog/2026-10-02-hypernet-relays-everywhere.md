# Hypernet: a relay on every world, routes planned daily, reach 0.5 AU

- **A relay round every planet and moon** of a settled system (TALLIS WORLD RELAY, a
  constellation: it sees past its own world), as well as the station, ports and gates.
- **Routes planned each day,** as the worlds stand at its start: every place that needs the net
  (the station, ports, gates, beacons) gets its quickest way to the backbone, hopping relay to
  relay, and only the relays on those ways are switched on; the rest stand by (dim on the map).
  If a line on the day's routes closes mid-day (a world in the way), that branch finds another way
  at once.
- **Every relay product reaches 0.5 AU** (the backbone relay was 30 AU, the beacon 5 AU): reach is
  the product's spec.
- **The map draws each switched-on relay's reach to scale:** its true 0.5 AU circle through the
  chart's mapping (true angle; distance placed between the rings as the worlds' are).
- **What it means today:** the inner system is on the net hop by hop (Remeipei B, C, D: seconds
  to 6 minutes); from 1.1 AU out the gaps between worlds are wider than 0.5 AU, so those ports
  are dark: stronger relays, or relays between the worlds, would bridge them.
- The ring distances lose their "+".

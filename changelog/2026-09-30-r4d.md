# 2026-09-30 — Re-architecture R4c/R4d: records, the complete event log, causes everywhere

This completes R4: the services are out of the world, and every authoritative change records
its cause.

## Records (`services::records`)

- `Kill`, `TradeRecord`, `Deal` and `TrafficStats` moved out of the sim into `Records`, an
  observer that decides nothing.
- **Each kill carries its cause:** the wreck, as logged (`Cause::Event`).
- `Universe::{kills, trade_log, traffic}` became `Universe::records.{kills, trades, stats}`. The
  view and the game read them as before.

## The tick's event log, complete

- `Universe::log` now holds every ship event of the tick, in order: the player's, each craft's
  (in craft order), then the combat phase's.
- `logged(id, which)` turns a logged event into a `Cause`. It's used for:
  - kills;
  - traffic control's releases (a wreck, docking, a transit, a respawn);
  - write-offs of a lost ship's hold.

## Traffic control's journal

- **`TrafficControl::journal`:** every change (pad granted, queued, occupied or freed; corridor
  granted or freed) with its tick, ship and cause.
- **Causes:**
  - a pilot's request is a message from that pilot (`request_from`);
  - a release cites the logged event that ended the ship's business, or the pilot's own word
    (cancelling, fleeing);
  - presence changes are the rules acting on observed fact.
- The full-pads interaction test now checks the journal: the waiting ship's queueing and grant
  were at its request, and the pad was freed by the rules as its ship left.

## Boundary

`services/tests/boundary.rs`: services may read the charts, published specs, the contracts and
the core's reported state. Never the core's machinery: the `World`, damage, weapons,
collisions, applying rules, turret fire, stepping ships.

## R4 exit

- Every authoritative change has a cause record:
  - law rulings (event and evidence);
  - ledger entries (request or event);
  - traffic control changes (request, event or rules);
  - kills (event).
- The ledger balances (`Ledger::balanced`, tested).
- All 121 tests pass.

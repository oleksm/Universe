# Stargates in the registry; rings span 5, 10 and 25 light years

- **Ring spans changed:** Halcyon Ring I, II and III now span 5, 10 and 25 light years (were 10,
  25 and 50), in `content/base/structures.ron`. A gate of the seeded world is still the smallest
  class that spans its lane.
- **SFO 17, Stargates:** the three rings as registry records (opening 3 km, structure 120 m thick,
  span, capture speed), each a Halcyon product.
- **Worked out for each ring**, from Dogma's Tube laws (the same formulas as
  `crates/physics/src/hyper.rs`): the tube's weight, the energy and time to open it, the power to
  hold it, how many 400 MW fusion power stations that is, and what a 100 t ship, the MC-07, a
  hauler and a capital ship take and pay to cross it. Ring I: 12 GW to hold (31 stations); Ring
  II: 24 GW; Ring III: 61 GW.
- **A Stargates report:** each ring's span against the game's, and what a ring does not yet say
  (where its power comes from, what it weighs, what it is made of).
- **SFO 16, Ship equipment** (the same day): the thirteen modules the game fits to the MC-07.

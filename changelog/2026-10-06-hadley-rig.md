# Hadley Orbital Works in the world

- **Rigs are bodies** (`world::rigs`, `BodyKind::Rig`, `Facility::Rig`): each of the registry's
  rigs in a low circular orbit round the body its record says it's at (1.6 of its radii, as a
  station: invented, a rig record gives no orbit). Hadley Orbital Works goes round Rime at 5,814 km
  every 3.3 hours.
- **No model, so a box at its size** (the user's rule): the registry gives a rig no size, so the
  box holds what it's built of, its modules' volume at half packing (worked out): Hadley's is
  207 × 207 × 414 m. Drawn as a faint fill with its edges and its name along its nearest top edge.
- **A ship comes alongside by hand:** it rests on the rig's top, slowly (as on a station's deck);
  its sides bounce it off. No traffic control, no clearance, no autopilot to it; the hyperdrive and
  the follow program take it there.
- Its modules give no masses, so it weighs a stand-in 100,000 t.
- Dev scenario `rig`: the rig from a little way off.

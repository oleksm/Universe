# Imported ships: drawn where their physics is, lit as everything else

- **Offset:** a hull's shape is centred on its centre of mass; its glTF model is in the frame it
  was made in, so it was drawn off its physics. `Shape::made_centre` records the shift; the model
  is drawn shifted back.
- **Too dark out of the sun:** the PBR shader now takes exactly the meshes' light (the sun in
  shadow or not, the reflecting planet's through the eye's adaptation curve, the ambient floor),
  so a model in a station's shadow, on the night side or seen from below reads like everything
  around it instead of going black.

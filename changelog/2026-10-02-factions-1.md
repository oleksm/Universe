# Factions 1: the powers and their territory

- **Factions are world content** (`content/base/factions.ron`): Halden Concord (HCD), Free Reach
  Assembly (FRA), Tessaly Directorate (TSD), each with a name, a three-letter tag, a colour and
  what it stands for. The world needs at least one.
- **Territory** (`world/src/factions.rs`): the first faction sits at the home system; each other's
  seat is a settled system as far by gates from the seats before as can be; every settled system
  goes to the nearest seat. In the base world: Concord 3 systems, Reach 1, Directorate 1.
  `Charts::holder(system)`.
- **Shown:** the HUD's top line ("REMEIPEI (K)  HOME   HALDEN CONCORD SPACE", in its colour); the
  system map's title; **FACTIONS** (H) on the galaxy map, a layer: held systems ringed and tagged
  in their holder's colour, the factions listed with how many systems each holds.
- Plan: `docs/factions.md` (standing, faction law and security, enlisting, founding next).
- Dev scenario `galaxyfactions`. The gate network test checks every settled system is held, the
  home by the first faction, and every faction holds some.

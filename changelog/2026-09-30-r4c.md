# 2026-09-30 — Re-architecture R4c: traffic control as a service

- **`services::atc`** (moved out of the world) is traffic control:
  - pads, corridors and their queues (`TrafficControl`, `Presence`), from `world::pads`;
  - the clearance rules (`request`, `lapsed`, `nearest_station`), from `world::traffic`;
  - their tests, with them.
- **`World::traffic` is gone.** The universe owns the service (`Universe::atc`).
  - The player's bus (`Link`) and requests made outside the tick get exclusive access, so
    they're answered at once.
  - Crafts stepping side by side see it shared, as it stood at the tick's start
    (`FrameLink::atc`). Their requests are made after, in craft order.
  - The per-frame presence, releases on events and when fleeing, and the view's pad owners all
    go to the service.
- **The world keeps only what's physical or charted:** `Facility` (a place),
  `clearance_range`, `docked_at`, `facilities`.
- All 120 tests pass. Clearance and the pad assignment show in the game (`padview`: "LAND ON
  PAD 5").

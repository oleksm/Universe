# 2026-09-30 — Radar: detect and lock ships

Request: "detect ships around us and lock their info, maybe within a range of a ship. More like a
radar feature."

## How it's layered

The first feature built on the new architecture:

- **world, the radar device** (`world/src/radar.rs`): a sensor. `sweep(own, system, others)`
  returns `Blip`s (id, position, velocity, distance), nearest first. It sees ships within
  `RADAR_RANGE` in the same star system that are physically present: flying, landed or docked.
  Ships in gate transit or wrecked don't show, and it sees nothing while its own ship isn't in
  normal space. `Blip::closing_speed` gives the range rate. It measures; it doesn't identify.
- **avionics, the lock**: `Avionics::contact`, the radar id the nav computer is locked on (not
  saved).
- **sim, contacts** (`sim/src/contacts.rs`): the radar's blips plus each craft's *transponder*:
  its name, what it's doing (DOCKING / LANDING / GATE RUN / HYPERDRIVE / DEPARTING / AT STOP /
  PARKED / UNDERWAY), and its next stop. `Universe::contacts()`, `lock_next_contact()` (the next
  one out by distance; past the farthest it unlocks) and `locked_contact_in()`.
- **game**:
  - T (pilot) locks the next ship, nearest first.
  - The HUD line reads `LOCK <NAME> <range> CLOSING/OPENING <speed> REL <speed>`, followed by
    `<ACTIVITY> -> <DESTINATION>`.
  - The locked ship gets a cyan diamond bracket with its name and range, like the nav target's,
    with an edge arrow when it's off screen.
  - The scanner now shows only radar contacts, with the locked one boxed.
  - The lock drops with "RADAR CONTACT LOST" when the contact leaves range.
  - A new dev scenario, `radar`, flies alongside a settler with it locked.

## Range

`RADAR_RANGE` = 500 km, the distance the scanner already showed ships at. It was 100 km at first,
but in a probe after 6 game minutes the nearest of 21 settlers in the home system was 17,700 km
away. Settlers spread over a whole system, so a short-range radar would almost never see anyone.
500 km covers a station's or port's neighbourhood. A radar module with more range is an obvious
upgrade later (see nav modules as purchasable).

## Not yet

- Contacts are only the settlers' crafts (there are no other players yet).
- No "fly to contact", match velocity or intercept. The lock is information only, in keeping with
  manual flight by default.
- Line of sight isn't checked: the radar sees through planets.

Tests: `radar::tests` (range, system, visibility, ordering, closing speed) and
`contacts::tests` (lock order, unlock past the last, lost out of range). Clippy clean, all tests
pass.

## Follow-up: mark every contact

User: "see it on the radar but no marker on my hud". Only the locked ship had a bracket, so ships
the radar saw, but that weren't locked, showed on the scanner but not in view. Now every radar
contact in view gets a small cyan square, with its range underneath when it's within 50 km. The
locked one keeps the full bracket with its name. New dev scenario: `contacts` (nothing locked).

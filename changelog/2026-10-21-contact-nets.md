# Resolve authored wiring into static contact nets

The authored wiring report now compiles contact-level topology independently of model files and placement. Saved conductor assignments join declared equipment contacts; only registry `internal.kind: joined` bus bars add internal conductive links. Roles, line identifiers, channels, voltage and direction must agree. Isolated contacts remain isolated, spare contacts are excluded, and duplicate port use, dangling contacts, incomplete maps and invalid internal references withhold the topology. Wire order does not change net membership.

Switched groups retain their individual contact poles; converters retain their directed input/output port references. Neither is flattened into copper or assumed powered. The report names unresolved breaker states and conversion power transfer, and gives its static contact-net count. Cable/connector compatibility, current/drop, bends, supports and physical mounting remain separate checks. Flight compilation still rejects authored wiring; this is not operational runtime power.

Validation: six new regression cases cover explicit/internal connectivity, disconnected contacts, deterministic grouping, switched/conversion boundaries, invalid saved maps, multiple signal lines, power polarity/voltage/direction, and published internal records without model files (some scenarios share cases). `capped cargo test -q --workspace` passed; the game suite includes 28 passed and 3 ignored. The installed `registry_board_to_navigation_route` test passed explicitly with `--ignored`. `capped python3 tools/standards/build.py` passed with no generated changes. No real commuter save was read or modified by these checks.

Registry confirmed October 21 that cradle v2, deck v1, support shoe v1 and run-clip v1 port geometry passes, but the strut-clamp lug datum and actual mounting strength/stiffness remain unqualified. The 28 V board/cable gap is unchanged: no atmosphere, circuit derating or new schema has been assumed.

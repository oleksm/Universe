# Developer settings read one way (audit phase 4, #12)

- The engine's per-frame reads of `UNIVERSE_HITCH_MS`, `UNIVERSE_SCREENSHOT_AT`,
  `UNIVERSE_SCREENSHOT_FRAMES`, `UNIVERSE_MAX_FPS` and `UNIVERSE_SHADOW_DEBUG` are read once at
  start (`engine::devflags`): each read took the process's environment lock, one on the render
  thread every frame.
- The game's scenarios read through `devenv` (`num`, `flag`, `list`) in place of five copied
  `env` closures and the comma-list parsers.
- Two names meant two things; renamed: `UNIVERSE_FRAME` (a world's history frame, the `worlds`
  scenario) is `UNIVERSE_HISTORY_FRAME`, and in the shipyard `UNIVERSE_FRAME_TOOL` (the FRAME tool
  in hand); `UNIVERSE_DECKS` keeps its count (decks stacked) and the deck studio's flag is
  `UNIVERSE_DECK_STUDIO`. (`UNIVERSE_DIST` is still metres in some scenarios and radii in
  `planet`: kept, as captures use it.)

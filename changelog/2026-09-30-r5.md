# 2026-09-30 — Re-architecture R5: turrets as clients

User: "Lets continue with the turrets and whats remaining phase".

## The core: a turret is hardware

- **Each turret's gun** (`TurretGun`) points along a world direction and turns toward its
  ordered aim at `TURRET_SLEW` (4 rad/s). While its trigger is held, it fires the same slugs a
  ship's gun does at `TURRET_RATE` (5/s), along wherever it actually points.
- **`TurretCommand { aim, fire }`** (in `universe-protocol`) is its actuation:
  `World::command_turret`. Gunners read the gun's state back with `World::turret_gun`.
- **Gone from the core:** target choice, the line-of-fire check, tracks and the lead
  (`TurretTrack`, `fair_game`, `turret_tracks`), and the law's standing on `Armed`.

## The gunner (avionics `gunner`): a client

- **It sees** the turret's position and how its gun points, the fair-game ships around it (from
  the law's standings, as the frame's snapshot has them), the line of fire (published charts and
  the physics kernel's ray), and gravity.
- **It acts:** it picks the nearest ship it has a clear line on, tracks it (fire control's
  `Track`, for acceleration), leads it (less gravity), lays the gun, and fires once the gun is
  within `ON_TARGET` (0.004 rad) of the lead.
- **It knows its latency.** What it sees is a tick old, and its orders land after the command
  delay. So it leads from where it and its target will be then.

## The defence service's pool (sim)

- **Each tick,** in every system with someone fair game in it (flying or landed), each turret's
  gunner orders its gun. Orders go through the command delay like a pilot's
  (`Universe::turret_orders`). Where no one's fair game any more, the gunners stand down.
- **Fixed on the way:** the gunner must see the turret at the same moment as its targets (the
  snapshot's). With the turret at the frame's end and the targets at its start, everything
  orbiting at ~50 km/s put the lead ~800 m off, and every round missed.

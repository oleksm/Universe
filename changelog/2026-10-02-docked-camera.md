# The camera stays with you when you dock

- **Docked at a station, the camera now keeps your view** (chase or cockpit, as set) and your
  ship is drawn as in flight.
- **Before**, it backed off 1.8 km: a leftover from the slot station, where the docked ship sat
  inside the station and could only be seen from far off. On the open deck there's no need.

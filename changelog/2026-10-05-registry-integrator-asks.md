# Registry: the integrator's asks, first part

*Registry only (`fso`).*

- **Goods say what they are traded as** (`identity.traded_as`), like every other physical record.
- **Farmland can be zoned** (`agricultural`) and farms, food works and stores can be facilities.
- **Open-sky modules say so,** and say which inputs the world itself gives (carbon dioxide, water).
- **The hydroponic hall says what temperature it holds,** so its power can follow where it stands.
- **Made things begin to say how long they last:** eight goods so far.
- The Measures are laws of kind `reference`; the food companies are of business `food`; six moon
  descriptions follow their new temperatures.

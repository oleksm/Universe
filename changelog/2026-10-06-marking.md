# 2026-10-06: marking (SFO 21)

- Every made thing carries a mark: a **serial** for units (hulls, equipment, modules, structures,
  parts: ticker, product code, six-figure sequence; the design, the maker who built it, the works,
  the world time finished, the serials of marked parts within) and a **batch** for lots (stock,
  goods; the heat for mill stock). Common schema `mark` and `batch`; day-0 stock may carry
  `serials` and `batch`; the Trethi yard's nine hulls are marked. The game stamps at production.

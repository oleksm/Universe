# 2026-09-30 — Routes and the route autopilot (groundwork for settlers)

## Summary

A ship can now fly a list of destinations on its own, across star systems: leave, cross
systems through gates, travel by hyperdrive, dock or land, wait, and continue. Settler routes
are generated reproducibly from a seed, for stable simulations.

## Player-facing

- **Nav map (M)**: browse any system of the gate network with ←/→ (title shows `< 1/5 >`,
  "YOU ARE HERE" or the distance in light years). **A** adds the selected station or spaceport to
  the route, **Backspace** removes the last stop, **C** clears the route, **G** loads a settler
  route (a new seed each press). Stops in the viewed system are marked `+`; the route is listed
  with the current stop highlighted. Enter still locks a target (in your own system); Del clears it.
- **K** with a route set starts/stops the **route autopilot** (otherwise K is the dock/land/gate
  or hyperdrive autopilot as before). While it flies, your controls are hands-off.
- HUD: `ROUTE 4/10 -> PORT LEBECE (USSODIVE)  AUTO: HYPERDRIVE`; stages: CLIMBING, HYPERDRIVE,
  GATE RUN, GATE TRANSIT, DOCKING, LANDING, AT STOP - LEAVING IN m:ss. Messages and sounds for
  each stop, completion, or a blocked route. Routes are saved with F5.

## Simulation

- `route` module: `Stop { system, target }`, `Route { stops, next, active, dwell_until, departing }`,
  and `gate_path` (breadth-first search over gate links).
- `Universe::route_step` (runs every step when active): at the stop → dwell 20 game s, then leave
  (launch from a station; lift off a surface and climb 3 km first); flying → next hop is the stop
  or the next gate on the path; far → hyperdrive with its autopilot; close → request clearance
  and let the dock/land/gate autopilot fly it.
- The hyperdrive autopilot now swings around any planet in the way (not just the target's), and
  the manual "planet dead ahead" drop-out doesn't apply to it.
- `Universe::settler_route(seed, count)`: a shuffled pick of stations and spaceports across the
  5 gate-network systems; same seed → same route, no repeats while there are enough destinations.
- Tests: gate paths; reproducible settler routes; a 3-stop route (land at the home port, dock at
  the home station, cross a gate and dock in the next system). An ignored long test flies a whole
  10-stop settler route (4 systems, 7 gate transits, 8 landings, 1 docking) in 5.3 game hours,
  headless in 0.12 s: `cargo test -p universe-sim --release settler_route_flies -- --ignored --nocapture`.

## Dev

- Scenarios: `routemap` (map with a settler route), `route` (route autopilot mid-route).

## Traffic: many ships, settlers

- **Crafts**: the `Universe` now holds other ships (`crafts`), each with its own ship state,
  system, route, neighbouring stars and event log. `step_world` steps the player's ship and then
  every craft from the same moment (the clock moves once), by swapping the craft's state into the
  same, already-tested single-ship code: physics, docking/landing/gate autopilots, hyperdrive,
  route autopilot.
- **Shared per-frame planet snapshots**: every ship stepping in the same system at the same moment
  reuses one ephemeris.
- **Settlers**: `spawn_settlers(count, seed)` starts each docked or landed at the first stop of its
  own reproducible 10-stop route, with departures staggered over 10 game minutes; when a route
  is finished, it gets a new one (next seed). Traffic totals: stops, gate transits, completed
  routes, crashes, plus a crash log with what each ship was doing.
- **Fix found by the traffic runs**: 4 of 5 early crashes were ships flying straight lines across a
  planet's moon system into a *different* moon (the landing approach only minds its target), and
  1 clipped a gate ring on the way. The route autopilot now keeps using the hyperdrive (which
  steers around bodies) until 200 km from a port / 30 km from a station or gate. Result: 0 crashes.
- **Measured** (release, one core): 100 settlers for 10 game hours → 1,763 stops, 1,875 gate
  transits, 141 routes completed, 0 crashes, 0.24 ms/frame. 1,000 settlers with 934 flying at
  once → 0 crashes, 2 ms/frame. Same seed → identical world (tested).
- **Game**: `UNIVERSE_SETTLERS` (default 50). Other ships are drawn in cyan (wireframe close up,
  a dot far away, names within 20 km), appear as blips on the scanner (within 500 km), and the
  HUD shows `TRAFFIC 50 SHIPS, 12 HERE  STOPS … GATES … CRASHES …`. In observer mode **T**
  follows the next settler (its route stop and what it's doing). Ships don't collide with each
  other yet, and settlers aren't saved with F5 (they're regenerated from the seed).
- Tests: `settlers_are_reproducible_and_move`; long runs: `settlers_fly`, `autoland_on_every_moon_port`.
- Scenarios: `traffic`, `follow`.

## Autopilot hyperjumps to far targets (landing from orbit: over an hour → minutes)

- Playtest: "if I start landing it shows me 1+ hr so I have to manually re-warp closer".
- The dock/land/gate autopilot (K) now first covers long distances by hyperdrive (with its
  autopilot), like the route autopilot: beyond 200 km from a port or 30 km from a station/gate
  it jumps, drops out next to the target at rest relative to it, and carries straight on with
  the landing/docking. Only when you've pressed K; manual flying stays manual.
- Landing from the station's orbit: 8,947 → 618 game s; from the far side of the planet:
  7,049 → 1,283 s; full 10-stop settler route: 5.3 → 3.5 game h.
- Bugs found on the way (all in the hyperdrive autopilot's way around planets):
  - aiming at a point on the far side of a planet spirals into it (a straight line to such a
    point always starts off heading inward); it now flies tangentially along the curve and holds
    a safe radius (at least 1.5 radii) until the direct line is clear;
  - the "what's in the way" check picked the body nearest the ship (sometimes a moon, whose
    detour led into the planet); it now takes the first body met along the path;
  - exactly opposite the target the detour direction was undefined; it now picks a side;
  - safety net: the hyperdrive drops out rather than move into any planet, moon or star.
- The flight planner now counts the thrusters that the autopilot adds to a main-engine burn,
  and models turning like the attitude controller (fast, then easing in), with the implied
  rotation rate; plans made 10 s apart agree within 5% (landing) / 0% (docking).
- Traffic check afterwards: 100 settlers × 10 game h → 1,868 stops, 0 crashes; 1,000 settlers
  flying → 0 crashes, 2.3 ms/frame.

# Review integrated landing gear before detail

Explorer bay clearance shrouds became detailed hanging exterior boxes before whole-ship silhouette review. Recolouring preserved the rejected geometry.

G2/G3 now require an integrated hull/gear/bay/support blockout with deployed, stowed and transition silhouette/clay views, ground plane and contact heights, plus the ramp relation. The identity, assembly and construction checks require a current whole-ship judgment before detail. A rejected shape returns affected packaging to G2/G3 and invalidates dependent evidence while preserving applicable product and crew/ground/load envelopes.

These are reusable review definitions. They do not accept the current Explorer geometry or implement an automatic aesthetic judge.

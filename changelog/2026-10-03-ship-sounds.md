# Ship and world sounds: deep and layered, not retro chirps

The cockpit computer's chirps (clearance, nav target, autopilot, route) stay; what the ship and
the world do no longer sounds like a sine gliding up an octave or three.

- New voices in the synth:
  - **sweep**: something huge moving: noise through a band gliding (and a resonance riding it),
    partials gliding with it, a sub beneath; building up to its end or dying from its start;
  - **resonate**: a great structure ringing (deep inharmonic modes, seconds long);
  - **servo**: a geared motor (its tone through a resonance, a little grit);
  - **beam**: the beam weapon's supply hum, plasma sizzle and crackle rising with heat, rounded off
    as heard through the hull (was the drone pitched up).
- **Gate in:** the ring's structure rings deep, the tube takes hold (a rush building to a boom),
  the flow roars on. **Out:** a boom, the rush dying away, the twin's ring behind (was a
  120→1800 Hz glide).
- **Hyperdrive on/off:** the field forming (a rush building, the frame taking it with a thud and a
  rattle) and letting go (was a 150↔1400 Hz glide); its drone now has the field's hiss, ebbing.
- **Weapons:** arming runs the covers' servos with the latches; hot, a clunk and the capacitors
  taking charge; safe, the charge draining and the servos back (were 150→700 Hz, a 1320 Hz beep,
  600→200 Hz).
- **Systems spooling:** a turbine (shaft and blade harmonics, the air) under the relays.
- Measured: half the energy of each now under 30-225 Hz (the beam under 1 kHz).

## NPC pilots apart from the world (`sim::pilots`)

- **The split.** A craft is now its body (`Craft`: ship, inbox, published `Status`) and its
  `Pilot` (avionics, feed, orders), held by the `Pool`. The world reads only the status (the
  transponder and flight plan) and talks to the pilot only by message: its ship's events, and
  its operator's orders (`Order::Route`, `Then`, `Flee`; nav target, clearance and autopilot
  for dev tools and tests).
- **The tick** applies postings due, steps every craft (physics only, side by side), handles
  what came of it in craft order, then hands the pilots the world as it now is (`PilotView`).
- **Postings.** A pilot thinking on the view of tick N posts its commands, its turn, its
  traffic requests, its events and its status, due at N + 2 (`COMMAND_DELAY`, the decided k).
  A posting takes effect whole at its due tick, however early it came. A late one takes effect
  at once (counted); a stale one (more than 30 ticks late) is dropped (counted).
- **Apart.** In the game the pool runs on its own thread and worker pool (half the cores) and
  thinks on the newest view whenever it can; the world never waits. `UNIVERSE_LOCKSTEP=1`, and
  the tests, think in step. The F3 panel shows `PILOTS APART LATE n DROPPED n`.
- **Think rate.** Every tick while flying (by hand or program) or busy (clearance, queue, hunt,
  follow, leaving); every 0.5 s coasting; parked, when its stop is up (at least every 5 s).
- **Dead-man rule.** A flying ship whose pilot posts nothing for 30 game seconds has its engine
  and thrusters cut and its weapons made safe.
- **A pilot sees its ship with its own commands on their way** (engine, thrusters, hyperdrive,
  master arm), so it doesn't order again what's coming.

## Bugs the 2-tick delay exposed (NPCs flew with none in the game until now)

- **A late steering order restarted the hyperdrive** after it had dropped out, and nobody
  steered it. Hyperdrive orders now carry `start`: only the engage control switches the drive
  on; steering orders only fly an engaged drive.
- **The pilot cleared its own hyperdrive autopilot** on hearing "drive engaged", which now
  arrives after the autopilot has taken over. `toggle_hyperdrive` already does that when the
  order is given.
- **Dropping out of hyperdrive left the ship a tick behind its planet** (the bodies had moved
  on): 3.5 km underground at 84 km/s. A drop-out now carries the ship along with its frame first.
- **The departure climb ended inside the drive's interlock**, so it engaged and dropped out
  over and over while falling. It now climbs until the drive can run (`clear_to_jump`; the
  world exports `hyperdrive::INTERLOCK`).
- **The drive near planets flew in the target's frame**, so the planet swept into the ship at
  their difference in speed (a gate's orbital speed round its own planet was enough). Within 10
  radii of a body and short of the target, it now keeps the body's frame.
- **Pilots queued to leave thought only every 5 s.** Parked with its stop over, a pilot now
  thinks every tick.

# Docked mode: the pilot's own business

- **Nothing happens by itself when you set down any more:** no automatic refuel and no automatic
  repair.
- **Set down, the panel is DOCKED (at a station) or LANDED (elsewhere):**
  - **J POWER UP / POWER DOWN:** the flight systems.
  - **P FUEL UP:** shows how much the tank takes (kilograms for a top-up); greyed out with the
    tank full or with no market here.
  - **N MEND HULL:** shows how sound the hull is; at a station, greyed out when it's sound.
  - **SHIFT+E LIFT OFF** (once powered up), **U AUTOPILOT**, **F FOOT**.
- Asking anyway says why not: "REFUEL: TANK FULL", "REPAIR: THE HULL IS SOUND", "REFUEL: NOT AT
  A PORT OR STATION".
- NPCs refuel and repair at their stops through the same requests as you.
- The keys have a scope of their own, docked or landed (every letter of REFUEL and REPAIR is
  taken in flight: FUEL UP takes P, MEND HULL takes N).

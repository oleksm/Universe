# The lab's clouds on screen

- **Merged** `planet` (the lab's clouds, `shaders/clouds.wgsl`, and its ground fixes: no polka dots
  from orbit, snow sliding off bare steep ground) and `fso` (Harvest's surface v4 with its clouds;
  Halden Camp, the first mine on a baked deposit; a taker for every by-product; `life` on every
  product).
- **Clouds wired:** a world's three cloud maps from its bake (`worlds::Heights::clouds`: by month,
  El Niño's change, the air they sit in; RGBA8 read exactly, group 2 bindings 12 to 14) and the
  clouds now (binding 15: the year's phase in the world's own months, El Niño's index from its
  series, world seconds; one epoch, world time 0, for every player: `worlds::clouds_at`). The bound
  world's clouds over its ground (after its air), over its sky (the sky pass), and shading its
  sunlit ground. The world's turn rides in `Globals::world_to_body`. The engine asks the GPU for its
  own count of sampled textures (past the default 16 now).
- GPU time: 1.0 ms from orbit and under overcast at 1 km.

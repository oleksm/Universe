# A score

- **Generative, sparse, under everything:**
  - pads (three detuned saws a note, low-passed, slow to come and go) drifting through a dark
    minor cycle (i, VI, iv, v in D), 14 s a chord;
  - now and then a bell (FM) from the chord, an octave or two up;
  - all of it through the long room.
- **Tension** (weapons armed, a missile tracking you, the hull under half): the pads darken,
  the bells thin out, and a pulsing bass on the chord's root fades in (eighth notes at 96 a
  minute).
- **Arrivals** (out of a gate, into a system, docked at a station, landed at a port): a swell
  for a few seconds, the pads opening up.
- **F10 turns the music on or off** (F8 still mutes everything). Silent while paused.
- `Audio::set_music`, `music_swell`; calm and tense added to the offline sound test.

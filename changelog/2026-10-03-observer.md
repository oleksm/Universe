# The observer: recordings and a live port (docs/observe.md)

- **Recording:** F3 debug on (a DEBUG button on the top bar now), SHIFT+F3 (PROFILE) records up
  to 10 s: every frame as a PNG, each frame's time and scopes, a timeline trace of every thread
  (Chrome's format: Perfetto), the state at the start and end, what the renderer held, and a
  summary of the slowest frames. Kept in `sessions/`, the newest 3; older and day-old ones
  cleared at start-up.
- **The live port** (127.0.0.1:7878): status, frame times, profile, resources, graphics
  settings (read and set), a screenshot of the next frame, recordings started and stopped,
  sessions listed. For whoever's helping to look into the running game without restarting it.
- The profiler records a trace (scopes over 20 µs, per thread) while recording.
- Captures are compressed and written off the render thread (a shutdown waits for them).
- The renderer reports what it holds (meshes, instances, models, surface-map layers).
- The profile panel (F3 twice) stacks under the performance column and its graph (it sat at a
  fixed height over them), as many rows as fit; the news feed under it.
- **Quiet unless asked:** slow frames are logged and written only while debug's on (F3) or a
  recording runs (they flooded the log, loading included); `hitches.log` starts over past 1 MB.
  The live port opens the first time debug comes on and answers only while it's on.

# The air's drag: turbulent, dull, quiet

- Was noise through one band rising to 2.6 kHz with speed (a swish). Now turbulence: the flow
  buffets in two random walks (a slow one, about a second, and a fast churn, about 8 a second); a
  deep rumble pumped by the slow buffet; a dull rush with no resonance, never brighter than about
  1.2 kHz, churning; a broad roar band (250-600 Hz) where the flow tears off edges.
- Measured (moderate / high speed): about 10 dB quieter; 90% of its energy under 360 Hz / 1.35 kHz
  (was 1.1 / 3.5 kHz); its loudness swinging about twice as much (the buffeting).

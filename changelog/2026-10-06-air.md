# The lab's air on screen

- **Merged `planet`** (the user's word): the lab's ground material (rock by unit on steep and high
  ground, snow by temperature and height, sand on low coasts, wet valleys, scree), its air
  (`shaders/air.wgsl`: single scattering by Rayleigh, Mie and ozone, the sunlight dimmed on its way
  in, the planet's shadow) and its sea (`shaders/sea.wgsl`, not wired yet).
- **The air wired:** a world's air from its bake (`atmosphere.json`: `worlds::Heights::air`,
  packed as the shaders' `Air`, binding 7 of the world's maps), bound with its maps. The world in
  view's ground and globe are seen through it (`air_ground` in `fs_mesh`, the placeholder haze for
  the others); its sky is the air's own light, drawn behind everything (a full-screen pass,
  `fs_air_sky`, on a black background where the world's air is bound). `Globals` carries the
  inverse view-projection and the world's centre and radius (`Frame::world_maps` takes them).
- To calibrate with the lab: the sky comes out dim against the ground (the ground's colour is
  halved by its globe brightness, the tone map's exposure is 0.5 with AgX where the lab's viewer is
  0.95 with ACES).
- Dev: `UNIVERSE_SCREENSHOT_AT=n` takes the screenshot at frame n (not 120), without every frame
  before it written down.

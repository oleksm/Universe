# A new pilot starts docked

- **First time in: parked on a pad of the home station's deck,** powered down, a full tank.
  Traffic control gives the pad and holds it like any landing.
  - "WELCOME TO … STATION - J POWERS UP, SHIFT+E LIFTS OFF".
  - Ships registered after (the settlers) take the next pad nobody holds.
- **A respawn (a ship lost) is as before:** 4 km behind the station on its orbit. It now gives
  up whatever traffic control held for the old ship (its pad, its corridor).
- The flight tests that fly from the old spawn start there explicitly (`respawn`, one step).

# The sun's core goes behind what's in front of it

- **The glare's core is now drawn in the scene,** just this side of the star, so a station's
  structure or a ship in front of it hides it pixel by pixel: half behind an edge, cut flat.
- The halo, rays and veil (lens effects, which do spill over an edge) stay over everything,
  faded by the share of the disc in sight.
- Checked in frames: `sunedge0`, `sunedge1`, `sunedge2` (the line to the sun 4 m under, on,
  and 4 m over the structure's top edge).

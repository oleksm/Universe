# Factions 4: enlisting

- **ENLIST (Z, docked at a station)** swears you to the station's holder: +10 with it. Docked at
  one of its stations, the same key leaves (-5). Refused while it finds you UNWELCOME or worse
  ("THE HALDEN CONCORD WON'T HAVE YOU"), or while you're sworn to another ("RESIGN AT ONE OF ITS
  STATIONS FIRST"). The docked bar shows "ENLIST HCD" / "LEAVE HCD". (Every letter of ENLIST is
  taken when docked: Z, pinned.)
- **Settlers enlist too:** a settler swears to the holder of the first station it stops at (the
  pilot's own call, the same request as the player's); pirates never do. In ten minutes of the
  home system's traffic, 17 settlers swore.
- **Membership counts:** a deed against a member (opening fire on it, destroying it) counts with
  its faction wherever the faction hears of it, not only in its space.
- **Shown:** members' transponders carry their tag ("SETTLER 90 [HCD]"); STAND says MEMBER; the
  galaxy map's factions layer says ", MEMBER".
- **Saved:** your oath and your standings go in the save (by faction key).
- Avionics events gain `Notice` (something done, said back). The refit test also swears in at the
  station and checks the oath survives a save. Dev scenario `sworn`.

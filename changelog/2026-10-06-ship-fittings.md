- **38 pieces of equipment** in five groups: access (airlocks, ramps, lift, bay doors, docking
  collar and clamp), the bulk path (scoop, conveyors, transfer arm), heat (radiators, exchanger,
  coolant loops, heat sink), liquids and gases (water, waste, cargo and gas stores; pumps,
  compressor, port, boom), hull and energy (viewports, battery, solar array, switchgear, reaction
  wheels). Each with a mount, parts and a complete chain. Three new slot kinds: access, handling,
  thermal; nineteen new function kinds (not made in the game yet).
- **Node forgings** as stock (4340, three sizes) from Trethi's forging press.
- The Trethi yard gains an electronics works (the fittings' controls).

# Freefall Facts: from rock to a finished hull

The registry branch (`fso`) is merged into `main` again. Since the first merge it gained:

- **The MC-07 hull and its parts**: 29 parts named after the model's objects, 26 parts of parts,
  each with mass, size, the mill stock it is cut from and how it is made (SFO 12, 13, 14).
- **The chain down to rock**: mill stock, the Trethi Mill (rolling, bar, tube, forging), the Trethi
  Smelter (aluminium), rock with published compositions, and meteoritic iron won from stony ore.
- **The shipyard**: plate work, machining, fitting and hull assembly; Trethi Yard on parcel 4
  (Tolland Yards). An MC-07 can be built there in 20 days at most.
- **Confidence**: every number says whether it is sourced, derived or invented; guesses put in so a
  figure is there are marked to review. Cited research is in `standards/sources/`.
- **Structure and shock**: the hull's design loads; its legs designed for a 3.05 m/s landing, the
  hardest they take (8.1 m/s) and the jolt aboard; the ground the ship can hover over and the
  settlements it can set down at; SFO 15 Shock; guessed shock limits on parts and stock.
- **Power**: Port Trethi's station built to four fusion modules, a line to each facility.
- **Reports**: chain, mass, structure, shock, power, volume, balance, materials, processes, goods,
  confidence, to review.

What the registry now says that the game does not yet follow: the MC-07's parts weigh 154 t (the
game's frame is 87.8 t), and a landing is 3.05 m/s by design (the game allows 30 m/s).

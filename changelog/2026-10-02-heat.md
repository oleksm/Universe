# Heat: an energy balance, and worlds' climates

- **The hull's skin is an energy balance** (`heat::heat`, with its `Surroundings`):
  - **in:** sunlight on its lit side (none in a world's shadow), a near world's infrared glow and
    reflected light, a leak of its own power use, and in air the flow's heating and exchange with
    the air;
  - **out:** radiation at εσT⁴.
  - At 1 AU from a Sun-like star it settles about −6 °C, in shadow about −40 °C; at rest in air, the
    air's own. Landed ships cool and warm too.
  - It's reckoned a second of game time at a time (a tenth in air), so it costs nothing measurable.
- **Worlds' climates** (`climate`):
  - a mean from radiative equilibrium with their star (`SOLAR_LUMINOSITY` is Dogma's), their
    albedo, and a greenhouse from their air;
  - day and night swings: an airless moon to its extremes (one close to its star: 285 °C at noon,
    −136 °C at midnight), air damping them (the Earth-like home world: 19 °C mean, ±5 K);
  - cooler toward the poles, and air cooling 6.5 K/km with height.
  - New world-sheet numbers: albedos, greenhouse, swing damping, lapse rate, night floor, paint
    absorptivity, internal leak, convection.
- **HUD:** the instruments show the hull's temperature, and the air's outside when in it; on foot,
  the air's (or ground's) temperature. The skin warning starts at 600 K, past anything sunlight does.
- **The HUD font has °, –, ·, ², ³, ×** (beyond ASCII, in the atlas's spare row).
- `UNIVERSE_DEBUG=1` starts with the debug info up.

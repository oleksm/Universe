# One newsfeed

- **The kill notifications are news:** the right-side feed is one newsfeed of what's come to us over
  the hypernet (or within our own comm's hearing): kill reports and outlets' digests together,
  newest on top, each for 15 seconds from when we heard it, with where it's from and how long it
  was on its way. Kills that were ours (we fired, or we were hit) stay red.
- **NEWS (F11)** is one timeline: reports and digests by when we heard them.

# Shared asset loader completion

Game modes share asynchronous registry/native model preparation, predictive demand and bounded CPU/GPU residency. Startup presents a responsive shell while simulation preparation runs. F3 and observer profiling expose loads, hits, unloads, reloads, queues, bytes and failures. Distant rear requests stop renewing residency; nearby or anticipated turns retain a conservative neighbourhood.

Final workspace, registry and Vulkan evidence is recorded in docs/diagnostics/asset-loader-finish-v1.json. Budgets account payloads, not total RSS; startup measurements distinguish shell submission from CPU readiness and include uncontrolled OS caches.

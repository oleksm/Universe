# The hyperdrive is navigation's

- Hyperdrive engages only in Nav mode: nothing fights or digs in hyperspace. In Combat or
  Mining mode the key says so; the HYPERDRIVE cell is gone from those panels.
- In hyperspace, Combat and Mining modes are refused ("NO COMBAT IN HYPERSPACE").
  (Weapons already couldn't fire there.)
- To escape a fight: B back to Nav, then the hyperdrive.

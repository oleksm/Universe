# Chemical engine energy consistency

CH S1/S2 and S3/S4 kept their stated exhaust speeds and fuel energy, but their derived efficiencies had been rounded down to two significant figures. The new Engine consumer therefore correctly excluded them from the catalogue: the declared jet energy exceeded the declared usable fuel energy.

Keep efficiency as exhaust squared divided by twice the registry fuel energy: 0.7446153846153846 for hydrolox at 4400 m/s and 13 MJ/kg; 0.541205 for methalox at 3290 m/s and 10 MJ/kg. The basis retains review status because the fuel energy remains provisional. The family generator now preserves the same calculation instead of rounding to two significant figures. Existing engine performance, masses, geometry and fuel records are unchanged.

Validation: registry schema/build checks, exact energy balance for all five chemical engine records, and engine page checks. This corrects an arithmetic inconsistency; it does not qualify an Explorer equipment allocation or landing/thermal capability.

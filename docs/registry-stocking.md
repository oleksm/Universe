# Stocking: how much lies in store, and why

2026-10-06. Three layers, each with its reason; day-0 stock is derived from them, never typed.

| Layer | Holds | Set by | Where it is written |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Working stock** | what the user of a thing keeps to hand: a works three days of its inputs, a store a week, a farm a season of fertiliser and feed | throughput | the facility's `stock` (the registry's convention; stated on the administration's `compulsory_stock`) |
| **Distribution stock** | a settlement's warehouse: `warehouse_cover` (1.5) resupply cycles of what its people take, so one late delivery does not empty it | geography: `settlement.resupply {from, interval}` | the warehouse's `stock` |
| **Compulsory reserve** | the administration's law: for the needs people die without, how long all its people could live on store alone, and where it lies | law: `org.<administration>.compulsory_stock.reserves` | the holding settlements' warehouses |

Treistun's law: **120 days of food and 90 of medicine for everyone in the system**, half at Port
Eikir and half at Port Nacaubun (the two big ports under open sky). These are Switzerland's
compulsory stocks (about four months of staples, about three of critical medicines; from memory,
marked review). Like zoning, the law can be broken; a breach is the administration's to record.

Resupply intervals are chosen by distance and owed a reckoning from the orbits: Heath's moon 3
days, Hoar 7, the inner worlds 20, Rime 30, the giants' moons 60.

What this gives at day 0: Port Eikir's warehouse holds 5,190 t of flour (its own week plus half the
system's four months); Port Aipika, 60 days out among the giants' moons, holds 90 days of its own
food (1.5 × 60) and no reserve; Port Sirnendis, three days from the granary, 4.5 days. Air and
water are not in the law: every sealed port makes its own, and keeps 30 days of oxygen and a week of
water as working stock beside its plants.

Generated by the stocking step (`tools/standards/stocking.py`; to move into
build.py as a check that each warehouse holds at least its layers).

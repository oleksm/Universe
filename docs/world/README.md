# World articles

How things work in the universe, one subject an article, written so anyone (players, modders,
the community) can follow every choice and argue with it.

## How a number earns its place

Every constant is one of these, and says which:

| Kind | Means | Must come with |
|---|---|---|
| **Real** | nature as measured | a citation |
| **Derived** | follows from other constants | the derivation |
| **Invented law** | the *form* of a rule we add to nature | the real-physics idea it's modelled on, and why that form |
| **Invented constant** | a value in an invented law | the **design target** it's chosen to meet, the calculation (a script in `tools/experiments/`), what changes if it's doubled, and the Dogma check that guards it |
| **Tuning** | a gameplay knob | flagged as such, with the experience it serves |

Invented numbers aren't justified by physics; they're justified by **stated design targets**
("no ship crosses even 5 ly", "data crosses a light year in 200 ms"). The targets are the
design, and what's open to debate; given them and the law's form, the constants are computed,
not picked. A number with no target, derivation or source is a bug in the article.

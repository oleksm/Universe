# The mining ship

What a mining ship is, from first principles: why it carries what it carries. (Worked out with the
user, 2026-10-03.)

## Its purpose

**To turn rock somewhere far away into value at a market, and bring its crew home.**

Every system aboard answers a force the world puts in the way of that.

## The forces, and what answers them

| Force (what the world imposes) | What it demands | Systems that answer it |
|---|---|---|
| **Distance**: the rock is far from the market | get there and back | drive, hyperdrive, propellant, navigation, flight computer |
| **Gravity and motion**: rocks spin, worlds pull | hold position, land, lift | lift and manoeuvring thrusters, gear, anchor clamps |
| **Rock is hard and mixed** | break it, find the good part | prospecting sensors, laser or drill, analyser |
| **Mass costs**: every tonne hauled costs propellant | carry only what pays | collector, crusher, refinery, tailings ejector, holds |
| **Energy**: cutting and moving takes power | make and store it | reactor, capacitors, power distribution |
| **Heat**: every watt used becomes heat; space barely takes it away | get rid of it | radiators, coolant loops |
| **Vacuum, time, danger**: people die out there | keep them alive, weeks at a time | pressure hull, life support, water, medical, quarters, galley, escape pod, fire and damage control |
| **Isolation**: help is far | fix things, call out | workshop, spares, comms |
| **Trade**: value is only real where it's sold | hand over cargo, prove what it is | docking, cargo handling, containers, transponder |

A mining ship differs from a hauler or a fighter only in which forces it fights hardest: rock,
mass and heat. That's why it carries the laser, the refinery and the radiators. Roles fall out of
the forces; nobody declares them.

## What follows

1. **A system earns its place when a force in the world makes it necessary.** If the game
   doesn't simulate the force, the system is decoration (a radiator matters only once heat limits
   what can run). The order: the force, then the system, then a standard where makers meet.
2. **A standard exists where two makers' things meet across one of these forces:** power into a
   module, heat out of it, ore into a hold, a ship onto a pad. The retractable hardpoint comes
   first because rock, energy, heat and mass all meet there.
3. **The interior's rooms are its compartments:** cockpit, quarters, galley, medical, life support,
   workshop, airlock, holds, refinery, reactor room.

## In the game now, and waiting

- **Simulated forces:** distance and propellant, gravity and motion, rock and mining, mass,
  energy, trade, vacuum on foot. Their systems are mostly in.
- **Not yet:** heat (radiators, coolant), life support over time, breakdowns and repair (roadmap
  5), refining and matter (roadmap 8). Their systems wait for them.

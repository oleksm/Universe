# A complete civilization

*The vision, 2026-10-04. Written without reference to what the game has: what a good civilization
is, so that what we build can be held against it. The plan that follows from it is
`docs/registry-civilization-plan.md`.*

A civilization is **sustainable when its loops close**, and **interesting when they can only close
through other people**.

## What people need, in order

People climb a ladder, and they care about a rung only once the one below is safe.

1. **To stay alive:** air, water, food, warmth, shelter, medicine.
2. **To live together:** a home, a family, safety, law, money, work.
3. **To get better:** schooling, a craft, tools, knowledge that grows.
4. **To matter:** something of their own, a name others know, art, play, belief, somewhere new to go.

A good civilization lets most people stand on the third rung and reach for the fourth. A bad one
keeps them fighting over the first.

## The loops that must close

| Loop | What goes round | What breaks if it does not close |
|---|---|---|
| Matter | Things are made, used, worn out and remade. One works' waste is another's input. | It runs out of something, or drowns in its own waste. |
| Energy | Fuel in, work done, heat shed. | The lights go out, and everything above stops. |
| People | Born, raised, taught, working, old, dead. | Too few hands, or too many mouths. |
| Knowledge | Every skill is taught to someone younger, or it is lost. | It can use its machines and cannot build them. |
| Money | Wages become spending, spending profit, profit investment and taxes, taxes roads and schools. | Wealth pools somewhere and stops moving. |
| Trust | Promises are kept because records, standards, courts and reputation make breaking them dear. | Nobody deals with strangers, so nothing large is built. |

Sustainability is the first five. The sixth is what lets them grow past a village.

## What moves people

The gap between where they are and where they could be, so long as it can be crossed.

- **Reward that differs.** Some work pays more because it is harder, rarer or more dangerous.
- **Ownership.** Land, a ship, a workshop that is yours, and can be lost.
- **Standing.** A name, a rank, a reputation that opens doors.
- **Fear of loss.** Hunger, debt, a failed harvest. With nothing at stake nobody strives.
- **Children.** People build for those who come after them.

## What makes life in it interesting

- **Difference.** No place has everything. One world has food, another water, the rocks have metal.
- **Dependence.** So every place needs the others, and the links between them are where the stories are.
- **Fragility.** A late ship matters. A full warehouse stops a factory. Things can go wrong.
- **Time.** Building takes days and growing takes seasons, so a decision has weight.
- **A frontier.** Somewhere with no rules yet, where the bold get rich or die.
- **Named makers.** Things come from someone, and you learn whose work to trust.
- **Tensions that never resolve.** Growth against limits, centre against frontier, company against
  commons, law against freedom, cheap against safe.

## What a complete one has

Everything on the ladder needs someone to provide it.

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Body** | farms, water, air, power, homes, clothes, clinics, a way for waste to go back |
| **Making** | mines, mills, works, builders, the makers of the machines that make, recyclers |
| **Moving** | ships, ports, roads, warehouses, messages |
| **Order** | administration, law, courts, police, records, standards, defence |
| **Money** | banks, exchanges, insurance, wages, taxes |
| **Mind** | schools, apprenticeships, research, archives |
| **Spirit** | art, sport, festivals, worship, taverns, travel for its own sake |

A place without the last two still works. Nobody would choose to live there.

## What it means for how we build

- **The physics comes first, and the story follows it.** What is possible is possible.
- **Nothing is made of nothing**, and nothing is only a number: every thing is somewhere, and can
  be seen.
- **The registry says what things are and the most they can do.** What is made, bought and done
  is the people's, and the market's.
- **Every action is one a player could take.** The seeded world is built the same way a player
  would build it.

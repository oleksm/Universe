## Challenges and dependencies

1. **Connected boarding geometry is missing or unverified.** The fresh Drover can exit via its normal hatch action, but reboarding has no interior floor destination. The live `Universe::walk` reproduction confirms this is not merely a missing harness layout. ORE CUTTER lands, then its walker stops at the closed cockpit boundary. The MC-07 assisted vertical landing trial crashes; its combined boarding geometry also remains held. A model having floor ray hits does not establish a traversable seat-to-ground-to-seat route.

2. **Terrain contact must match what is actually drawn.** Canonical physics is already shared. The remaining problem is rendering the same surface through loading, changing LOD, morphing and mixed patch ownership. Some settled contact probes improved, while crest and loading gates remained failed. Correcting individual tiles without their real incident neighbours can open seams; changing boundary morph after correction can invalidate a previously matched join.

3. **Memory and readiness must cover GPU lifetime.** A CPU mesh lease or a finished worker alone does not prove that an entire group is uploaded or within budget. The declared prototype caps are 16 MiB additional CPU working memory and 16 MiB additional GPU lifetime, with the existing two workers, 48 pending requests and 3,000 patch cap. These are experiment limits, not new geological or physical constants.

4. **Frame-rate evidence needs controlled display conditions.** The Studio rebuild removed measured CPU work, but some captures still spent about 26–51 ms waiting for the display surface while CPU/GPU work was small. Window title, workspace, focus and presentation conditions affected comparisons. This does not prove the renderer is universally fast or that every 30 FPS case is solved. Engine owns the controlled presentation diagnosis.

5. **Installed models and released main are separate.** Installation updates the asset store and registry branch; it does not automatically merge or rebuild main. The last merged cutoff is recorded and runnable. Later registry dimensions, masses and asset changes remain later changes until explicitly integrated. Neutral rendering also does not approve equipment motion, final fit, structure or thermal behavior.
